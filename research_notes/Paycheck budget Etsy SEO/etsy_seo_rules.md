## 1. Etsy's 2025 title guidance (what changed; how titles and tags divide the work)

### Takeaway
On 19 August 2025, Etsy published "New Guidance for Listing Titles, and a Tool to Help" in the Seller Handbook. It says:
- Name the item plainly.
- Put the most important traits (color, material, size) up front.
- Consider using fewer than 15 words.
- Don't repeat words.
- Move subjective words to the description.
- Leave out price, sale and shipping phrases.
- Include occasions and recipients only when they are essential to what the item is.

Etsy justifies the change by saying search now reads the whole listing: title, tags, attributes, description, first photo and reviews. The title therefore "doesn't need to do all the work."

### Cited Findings
- **Official article:** "New Guidance for Listing Titles, and a Tool to Help." The extract describes updated guidance for clear, buyer-friendly titles and an optional tool that suggests titles from existing listing data and can update several listings at once. The tool is "here to give you a starting point—not a directive. You're always in control of what gets published." — [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)
- **Date:** One summary dates publication to 19 August 2025. The Etsy Community Newsletter of 28 August 2025 also covers it. — [spctek summary](https://spctek.com/etsy-news-update-new-listing-title-guidelines/); [Etsy Community Newsletter Aug 28 2025](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Announcements/Community-Newsletter-August-28-2025/m-p/149000355)
- **Date conflict:** Some 2026 blogs say Etsy "rewrote" the title guidance in April 2026. I could not confirm a separate April 2026 revision. It may be a re-date or a refresh of the same article; the handbook page's metadata looked recently updated. — [ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-to-write-etsy-titles-that-actually-get-found-in-2026); [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-seo-guide-2026)
- **Core recommendations (Etsy wording, as quoted in extracts):**
  - State the item for sale clearly (e.g. "mug" or "dress").
  - "Include the most important traits upfront."
  - "Consider using less than 15 words when including the traits that make your item special."
  - Holidays, occasions or recipient types should be included "only if they are essential to what the item is, like 'birthday candle' or 'Halloween costume.'"
  - Move subjective words (e.g. "perfect", "beautiful") to the description.
  - "Try not to repeat words."

  Sources: [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697); [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/seo/etsy-title-update-2025-how-to-write-better-etsy-listing-titles/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-guidance-listing-titles-ai/)
- **Promotional phrases:** There is no need to add phrases like "on sale", "free shipping", "cheap" or "best price" to the title. Etsy shows price and shipping elsewhere in search results. — [spctek summary of Etsy guidance](https://spctek.com/etsy-news-update-new-listing-title-guidelines/); [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/seo/etsy-title-update-2025-how-to-write-better-etsy-listing-titles/)
- **Rationale (Etsy):** "Etsy's search system has evolved to take a more holistic view of your listing—including title, tags, attributes, descriptions, first photo, and even reviews." — [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)
- **eRank's example of repetition to remove:** "unisex adult poncho, unisex cotton poncho." eRank also says to move gift phrases such as "gift for him" and "birthday present" to tags or attributes. — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/seo/etsy-title-update-2025-how-to-write-better-etsy-listing-titles/)
- **Existing listings:** eRank reports that Etsy confirmed search does not penalize older-style titles. eRank calls the guidance optional and advises testing gradually rather than rewriting everything. — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/seo/etsy-title-update-2025-how-to-write-better-etsy-listing-titles/)
- **Printful's interpretation:** Etsy looks at the entire listing (title, tags, description, attributes), so there's no need to repeat the same words in every field. Printful also advises not to repeat phrases already covered by attributes or categories. — [Printful Help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/22448822724380-How-can-I-improve-my-Etsy-listings-with-the-new-Etsy-handbook)
- **Front-loading (practitioner claim):** The first 40-50 characters matter most because search results truncate titles. This is not from Etsy. — [ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-s-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026)
- **Conflicting practitioner advice:** Some 2026 guides still recommend using all 140 characters. This conflicts with Etsy's under-15-words suggestion. — [Rankhero/search summary](https://www.rankhero.com/blog/the-state-of-etsy-seo-in-2026-how-etsy-search-really-works); [ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-to-write-etsy-titles-that-actually-get-found-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Title rule for the build:** Item noun first, then 2-4 key traits, then format.
  - Format example: "Digital Download", "Printable PDF", or "Template for Goodnotes/OneNote".
  - Keep it under 15 words and roughly 70-100 characters, even though the limit is 140.
  - No repeated words, no adjectives like "perfect" or "best", no "sale" or "instant download sale".
  - Add a recipient or occasion only if it defines the item.
- **Example structure:** "Paycheck Budget Planner Template, Biweekly Budget Spreadsheet, Digital Download PDF".
  - The repeated "budget" is fine only if the phrase genuinely differs; ideally drop it.
- **Where the rest goes:** Long-tail synonyms and gift phrases move to tags.

## 2. Tag rules (13 tags, 20 characters, phrases, repetition, attributes and categories, plurals, misspellings)

### Takeaway
- **Limits:** 13 tags, each at most 20 characters.
- **Phrases:** Use multi-word phrases rather than single words.
- **Attributes and categories:** Etsy's own handbook says the categories and attributes you select act like tags. Don't spend tag slots on exact phrases already covered by your category or attributes.
- **Misspellings and regional spellings:** Etsy handles common misspellings and regional spellings (jewellery/jewelry). Don't tag deliberate misspellings.
- **Plurals:** Etsy has not stated a plural rule. Practitioners say search matches root words, so separate singular and plural tags are unnecessary.

### Cited Findings
- **Use all 13 tags:** The handbook recommends using all 13, since each tag is an opportunity to match a search. — [Etsy Seller Handbook (search summary)](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014)
- **Limits and allowed characters:** Up to 13 unique tags per listing, each up to 20 characters. Allowed characters are letters, numbers, hyphens, apostrophes and spaces; Etsy skips invalid tags. — [Printful Help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014067819-Are-there-limitations-for-Etsy-tags)
- **Phrases beat single words:** "The single tag 'recycled polyester' is better than two separate tags, 'recycled' and 'polyester.'" — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-tags/)
- **Categories and attributes act like tags (Etsy):** The handbook says the categories and attributes you add act like tags, so an exact phrase in your category doesn't need to be a tag. Its example: an item in the "Statement Ring" category doesn't need a "statement ring" tag. — [Etsy Seller Handbook – Keywords 101](https://www.etsy.com/dk-en/seller-handbook/article/keywords-101-everything-you-need-to-know/382774281517)
  - Date flag: Keywords 101 is an older, evergreen article. Its last update could not be confirmed, but it is still live on the handbook.
- **Fill every attribute (Etsy):** One handbook article says "each attribute you add acts like a tag," so it is worth filling every relevant option. — [Etsy Seller Handbook (search summary)](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014)
- **Misspellings (Etsy):** Etsy search redirects shoppers to the correct results after common mistakes, so deliberate misspelled tags are unnecessary. — [Etsy Seller Handbook – Keywords 101](https://www.etsy.com/dk-en/seller-handbook/article/keywords-101-everything-you-need-to-know/382774281517)
  - A 2008 community thread with anecdotal counter-evidence is dated and should not be relied on. — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Etsy-Success/Misspelling-tags-on-purpose/td-p/44401003)
- **Regional spellings (Etsy):** Shoppers searching "jewellery" will still see listings tagged "Jewelry." Some regional phrases are not mapped (e.g. Australian "thongs" for sandals) and may need their own tags. — [Etsy Seller Handbook – Keywords 101](https://www.etsy.com/dk-en/seller-handbook/article/keywords-101-everything-you-need-to-know/382774281517)
- **Plurals (practitioners only):**
  - Search matches root words ("earring" matches "earrings"), and singular tags save characters. — [Alura](https://www.alura.io/docs/article/etsy-tags-guide)
  - Separate singular and plural tags are not needed. — [EtsyEdge](https://etsyedge.app/blog/etsy-seo-tags-guide.html)
  - Etsy's handbook does not address plurals directly.
- **Tags vs. the title (practitioners, 2026):**
  - Tags should add variations and long-tail phrases the title doesn't cover, rather than repeat title words. Recommended length is 2-5-word phrases. — [ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/etsy-tags-best-practices-in-2026-the-full-playbook)
  - Repeating the same keyword across tags doesn't "stack." — [Marmalead blog](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-algorithm-2026/)
  - Vendors describe exact matches as a slightly stronger relevance signal than broad matches. Etsy has not confirmed this. — [Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-algorithm-2026/)
- **Attributes duplicating tags (practitioner):** Attributes and categories function as tags, so repeating them wastes space. — [AdNabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/etsy/etsy-tags/)

### Inferences
- **Recommended tag recipe for a digital planner or budget template:**
  - Use 13 distinct 2-4-word phrases of at most 20 characters, e.g. "paycheck budget", "biweekly budget", "budget by paycheck", "debt payoff tracker", "onenote planner", "digital budget".
  - Avoid duplicating the exact category name (e.g. "Templates" or "Planners") and any attribute values you selected.
  - Use singular forms.
  - Cover synonyms and buyer-intent and gift phrases that the shorter title no longer carries.
- **Older advice, now superseded:** Pre-2025 advice to "match your title phrases exactly in your tags for a boost" is older practitioner lore. The 2025 guidance (holistic search, no repetition) points away from deliberately duplicating title phrases in tags. Treat any title-tag overlap as incidental, not required.

### Gaps
- I found no official Etsy statement on plurals.
- I found no official Etsy 2025-2026 statement that explicitly says "don't repeat title phrases in tags." That rule is practitioner consensus.
- The exact list of attribute fields for the Paper & Party Supplies > Paper > Calendars & Planners category, or Templates, was not checked.

## 3. How Etsy search and Google use the description

### Takeaway
- **Etsy's guidance:** Etsy's handbook and Help Center say to describe the item clearly in the first sentence, because that sentence can appear in Google search results.
- **Keywords:** Description keywords are considered by Etsy and offsite search, but should read naturally and not copy the title.
- **The "160 characters" figure:** This is a practitioner rule of thumb (the Google meta description length), not Etsy's wording.
- **Weight in Etsy ranking:** Practitioners say the description carries only light weight in Etsy's own matching. Its main job is conversion.

### Cited Findings
- **Etsy Help Center (SEO for shop and listing pages):** "Clearly describe your item in the first sentence of your description." — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663987-Search-Engine-Optimization-SEO-for-Shop-and-Listing-Pages)
- **Etsy Seller Handbook:** "Pay special attention to first sentence of your description, because that will be displayed in Google search results." — [Etsy Seller Handbook – Give Your Titles, Descriptions and Tags a Makeover](https://www.etsy.com/ca/seller-handbook/article/28834504207)
  - Date flag: this is an older evergreen article.
- **Keywords in the description (Etsy, via podcast transcript):** Description keywords are considered by Etsy and offsite search. Add them in a way that sounds human, and avoid copying the title verbatim or simply listing top keywords. — [Etsy Seller Handbook podcast transcript](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1089151234475)
- **Descriptions are a matching input (Etsy, 2025):** Descriptions are named among the inputs to the "holistic" search view. — [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)
- **160-character claim (practitioner):** The first ~160 characters become the meta description Google shows, and roughly the same amount shows on the listing page before "expand." — [ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-to-write-etsy-descriptions-that-actually-sell-in-2026)
  - Etsy's own pages say "first sentence," not 160 characters.
- **Practitioner view of description weight:** Etsy "pays attention to a small amount of your description," but descriptions are mainly for converting buyers. — [Listadum/Craftybase via search summary](https://www.listadum.com/blog/what-is-etsy-seo-the-2026-guide-to-how-etsy-search-really-works)

### Inferences
- Write the first sentence to be at most about 155-160 characters.
  - It should state what the item is, the main keyword phrase, and that it is a digital download.
  - Example: "Paycheck budget planner template for OneNote — a digital download that helps you plan every paycheck, bills and savings."
- Follow it with scannable sections:
  - What's included
  - File formats and compatibility
  - How it works / delivery
  - Not included (no physical item)
  - Terms of use
  - AI disclosure, if applicable
  - Refund note

### Gaps
- Etsy does not publish how much description text its own ranking indexes.
- Etsy has not confirmed the exact Google snippet length.

## 4. Digital-product rules (disclosing digital downloads, refunds, compatibility, AI disclosure, "Made by" vs "Designed by")

### Takeaway
- **Creativity Standards labels:**
  - Digital downloads fall under "Designed by a seller."
  - "Made by a seller" covers physical items the seller makes.
- **AI disclosure:** AI use must be disclosed in the listing description. AI prompt bundles are not allowed.
- **File limits:** Up to 5 files per listing, at most 20MB each. Instant downloads are available to the buyer immediately after purchase.
- **Refunds:** Etsy makes sellers responsible for their own refund and return policies.
- **What I could not find:** No official Etsy rule requiring specific "this is a digital download" or compatibility wording turned up. That is best practice, supported by Etsy's general requirement that listings be accurate.

### Cited Findings
- **The four labels (Etsy Help, "What Can I Sell on Etsy?"):** "Made by," "Designed by," "Handpicked by," and "Sourced by" a seller.
  - "Made by a seller" means "physical items that are made by you, the seller."
  - "Designed by" covers digital items and seller-prompted AI creations, which "must disclose the use of AI."

  — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024112614-What-Can-I-Sell-on-Etsy)
- **AI disclosure in the description:** The Creativity Standards rule is reported as "Sellers must disclose within their listing description if an item is created with the use of AI." There is no prescribed wording; describe which part of the item involved AI. — [Growtsy](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy); [Listsy](https://listsy.app/guides/etsy-ai-disclosure-for-digital-products)
- **Conflicting claims about scope and dates:**
  - One source says an AI-image disclosure rule took effect on 1 March 2026 and applies only where AI was the primary tool for a photo. — [iscompliant/search summary](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-ai-digital-downloads)
  - Another says the rules were tightened on 10 June 2025. — [search summary of third-party compliance blog](https://flagproof.com/categories/digital-products)
  - Another cites a Seller Policy effective 9 July 2026. — [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards)
  - None of these were verified against Etsy text. The Creativity Standards were introduced in July 2024. — [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/is-ai-allowed-on-etsy-new-standards-clarify-whats-allowed/476821)
- **Example of a permitted AI-assisted planner:** A planner with AI-generated illustrations is permitted if the description says so and the listing is labeled "designed by," not "made by." — [izharshah (third-party)](https://www.izharshah.com/blog/ai-digital-products-labelling-rules)
- **AI prompt bundles:** These are banned or not eligible under "Designed by." — [aitrace summary](https://www.aitrace.org/company/etsy/practice/9868222b-5b23-41e2-9ab5-40b7cf6db2f6); [Growtsy](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy)
- **Originality bar (practitioner reading of Etsy standards):** Unmodified PLR, generic templates and bulk AI files don't meet Etsy's originality bar. — [search summary of iscompliant/krafie](https://krafie.com/blog/can-you-sell-ai-generated-products-on-etsy)
- **Listing accuracy (third-party):** Listing images should show what the buyer receives. The description should name the actual file types and package contents. — [iscompliant](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-ai-digital-downloads)
- **Digital file limits (Etsy Help):** Up to five digital files, with a maximum of 20MB each. Instant downloads are "ready-made files that the buyer can access immediately upon purchase." — [Etsy Help – How to Manage Your Digital Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Add-Files-to-a-Digital-Listing)
  - Some sites claim a 100MB limit; these appear unreliable.
- **Refunds (Etsy Help):**
  - "Each Etsy seller is responsible for their own policies regarding refunds, exchanges, and returns." — [Etsy Help – Refunds, Returns, and Exchanges for Sellers](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000572888-Refunds-Returns-and-Exchanges-for-Sellers)
  - Sellers aren't required to accept returns or give refunds unless their policy says so. — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015440807-How-to-Return-or-Exchange-an-Item-on-Etsy)
- **Digital downloads and returns (third-party):** Digital downloads don't offer return or exchange policies; the returns settings cover non-digital items. — [Alura](https://alura.io/docs/article/crafting-return-policies-on-etsy)

### Inferences
- **Production field:** Set "Who made it" to "I did" and the type to "Digital files", which maps to "Designed by a seller." Do not claim "Made by" for a template.
- **AI disclosure sentence:** If any artwork, copy or mockups used AI, add a plain sentence such as "Some decorative graphics were created with the help of AI tools and edited by me."
- **Statements to put in the description** (best practice, consistent with Etsy's accuracy expectations):
  - "This is a digital download — no physical item will be shipped."
  - The file formats.
  - Compatibility, e.g. "Requires Microsoft OneNote (Windows/Mac/iPad)."
  - "Colors may vary by screen."
  - Personal-use license terms.
  - "Due to the nature of digital items, returns/exchanges are not accepted; please message me with any issue."
- **EU caveat (my inference):** EU consumer-law withdrawal rights for digital content may still apply. Etsy's EU case system exists for refund disputes. Wording should not claim buyers have no recourse.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full Creativity Standards / Seller Policy text, so the exact AI-disclosure wording and its 2026 effective date are unconfirmed.
- I found no official Etsy rule mandating "digital download" wording in titles or descriptions.
- I found no official Etsy compatibility-disclosure rule.

## 5. 2026 updates to Etsy search (AI search, Gift Mode, listing quality)

### Takeaway
**Confirmed in Etsy's 2026 filings and earnings calls:**
- Search relevance and quality changes that surface higher-quality items.
- A conversational "Gifting Assistant", launched in beta in May 2026.
- Agentic-shopping partnerships with Google and Microsoft, plus a ChatGPT app. Traffic from AI agents is still under 1%.
- AI tools that help sellers describe, tag and price items.

**Not confirmed:** A seller-visible "listing quality score", a $6 shipping penalty, and a "Search Visibility Dashboard" are third-party claims I could not verify.

### Cited Findings
- **Gifting Assistant (Q2 2026):** "In May, the company launched the beta version of its new 'Gifting Assistant' to help buyers discover meaningful gift ideas through natural conversation." Usage skews to new, high-intent buyers. — [Etsy Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm); [Motley Fool Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/etsy-etsy-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- **Higher average order value:** AOV rose partly because search improvements surface higher-quality inventory. Management says it is not making relevance changes to drive AOV. — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/etsy-etsy-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/etsy-q2-earnings-call-lifts-140000175.html)
- **Agent traffic and partnerships:**
  - Traffic from AI agent platforms is under 1% of the total, but converts at a higher rate and larger order size.
  - Agentic shopping partnerships with Google and Microsoft were announced in January 2026, plus an Etsy app for ChatGPT.
  - On the seller side, AI is used to reduce the manual inputs needed to describe, tag and price a product.

  — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/etsy-three-way-ai-bet-starting-pay-off/); [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/04/30/etsy-marketplace-gms-mobile-ai-q1-2026/); [Etsy Q1 2026 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000042/q126shareholderletter.htm)

- **How search works (practitioner summaries of Etsy's "How Etsy Search Works"):**
  - Query matching uses categories, attributes, titles, descriptions and tags.
  - Ranking uses relevance, listing engagement, listing quality, customer service quality (messages, cases, reviews), shipping, recency and personalization.
  - New listings may get a temporary test boost.

  — [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/what-is-etsy-seo-the-2026-guide-to-how-etsy-search-really-works); [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-seo-basics/); Etsy's own page: [How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487) (not fetched)
- **Unverified 2026 vendor claims:**
  - US shipping over $6 reduces visibility.
  - Etsy launched a "Search Visibility Dashboard."
  - Keyword-stuffed titles now actively hurt ranking.
  - Micro-edits and tag reshuffling do nothing.

  — [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/etsy-search-algorithm-update-2026/); [ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-s-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026); [Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-algorithm-2026/)
  - The $6 shipping figure is irrelevant to digital downloads in any case.

### Inferences
- Because the Gifting Assistant and agentic or LLM shopping surfaces read listings in natural language, the following likely matter more in 2026:
  - Natural-language titles and descriptions.
  - Complete attributes.
  - Clear "what you get" text.

  This is consistent with Etsy's holistic-search statement but not stated by Etsy as a ranking rule.
- **For a digital budget planner, quality signals are what you can act on:**
  - A clear first photo.
  - Accurate expectations that lead to good reviews and few cases.
  - Fast message responses.
