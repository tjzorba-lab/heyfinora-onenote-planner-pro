# Competitor Etsy Listings: Paycheck Budget Spreadsheets / Budget-by-Paycheck Templates (Excel + Google Sheets), 2025-2026

> **How this data was gathered.** Direct fetches of etsy.com (listing and /market pages) failed: DNS was blocked from this environment, and so was the sidehustlenation.com case study. Everything below comes from **web-search result titles and snippets** of Google-indexed Etsy pages, gathered October 2026. Listing titles are the indexed `<title>` text, so they are verbatim but may be truncated. Prices, sales counts and badges are search-index snapshots and may be stale; several sale end dates had already passed. Bestseller badges could not be confirmed for any listing. No tag lists were visible, because tags don't appear in search snippets and eRank/EverBee/Alura need a login.

## Q1. Exact titles of top/popular listings, and which words appear first and most often

### Takeaway
Top titles are keyword-stacked strings of roughly 100-140 characters, with no brand words and almost no emotional copy. Nearly all of them **lead with "Paycheck Budget Spreadsheet", "Budget by Paycheck Spreadsheet" or "Budget Planner Google Sheets"**. Next comes the platform pair "Google Sheets ... Excel", then the pay frequencies "Weekly / Biweekly / Monthly", then 1-3 feature nouns: Bill Calendar, Debt Tracker/Snowball, Expense Tracker, Subscription Tracker.

### Inferences
- Titles spell the same concept several ways ("Bi-weekly", "Biweekly", "Bi Weekly"; "Paycheck Budget" and "Budget by Paycheck") to catch exact-match variants, even though Etsy guidance discourages repetition.

- Older 2022-2023 listings (IDs 10xx-14xx) still rank, which suggests accumulated reviews and sales outweigh newer title optimization. New entrants (IDs 44xx-45xx) add "(2026)" or a year to the title.

## Q2. How top sellers structure descriptions

### Takeaway
Descriptions are feature lists, not stories. A typical order: a one-line pitch, then the tab/feature list with tab count ("5 tabs", "8 tabs", "10 tabs", "12 fully connected tabs", "15 tabs"), then compatibility (Sheets vs Excel version), then how to access it (PDF with link), then tutorial/video, then personal-use and no-refund terms.

## Q3. Common differentiators

### Takeaway
The recurring feature set is: bill calendar, debt tracker or snowball, sinking funds, subscription tracker, an automated dashboard with charts, weekly/biweekly/semi-monthly/monthly pay options, any-currency support, and a video tutorial. ## Q4. Typical price points and bundle strategies

### Takeaway
Almost every listing runs a permanent "sale" of 40-75% off. Effective prices fall into three tiers:
- about $1-4 for low-cost single templates
- about $5-11 for mid-range paycheck systems
- about $14-23 for "Ultimate"/all-in-one bundles

The highest-volume sellers sit in the low and mid tiers. One case study reports that $20-30 templates made money only with ads.
