# Evergreen and Work/Productivity/Business Digital Products on Etsy (2022–2026)

> Method note: All findings come from web search result summaries (Oct 2026). WebFetch failed with DNS errors on every domain tried (rankhero.com, etsy.com, insightagent.app, offerengine.my, outfy.com, beehiiv.com), so I could not read full pages. Figures below are as the search tool reported them, and page-level context could not be checked. Etsy does not publish click-through rate, category GMS share or search volume. Every "search volume", "sales" and "revenue" number here is a **third-party estimate** (RankHero, Listify, EverBee/ProfitTree-style tools, blogs) or a **proxy** (listing counts, review counts, shop lifetime sales). EverBee itself claims about 80% accuracy, and independent reviewers dispute that figure ([OJ Digital Solutions EverBee review](https://ojdigitalsolutions.com/everbee-review/)).

## 1. Which work/business/productivity digital products have the highest demand and sales on Etsy (with numbers)?

### Takeaway
Digital planners are still the largest digital-download search category on Etsy. Resume/CV templates, Notion templates, Canva templates and spreadsheet (budget) templates come next. The proven top sellers are tablet (GoodNotes-style) planners and cheap Google Sheets/Excel budget spreadsheets, and a few listings or shops are estimated at six-figure revenue. All sales numbers are third-party estimates.

### Cited Findings
**Search-demand ranking (proxy)**
- In a March 2026 newsletter, the top Etsy digital-product searches were "digital planner" (19,770), "digital prints" (9,730) and "resume templates" (7,630). Notion templates (7,490) and Canva templates (5,320) were the top template platforms. The methodology is unclear. — [State of the Art newsletter, 2026](https://stateoftheart.beehiiv.com/p/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy-in-2026)
- RankHero estimates "digital planner" at about 27,100 average monthly searches and 693,616 Etsy listings (page updated Apr 2026). — [RankHero: digital planner](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner)
- Listify reports 1,044,640 listings for "digital planner", searches up 38% year on year, and demand peaking in January. The two tools' listing counts differ by about 50%. — [Listify: digital planner](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)
- RankHero estimates "notion template" at about 90,500 monthly searches, with difficulty 28/100 and most listings under $10 (updated May 2026). This figure conflicts with the 7,490 in the newsletter above, so the tools' methods are clearly not comparable. — [RankHero: notion template](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/notion-template)

**Sales and revenue estimates (case studies)**
- A single GoodNotes digital planner listing is estimated at **$894,000** total sales. This is a ProfitTree on-screen estimate inferred from public signals. The presenter has affiliate incentives, and the source calls it an outlier. — [ProvenStartups](https://provenstartups.com/projects/goodnotes-digital-planner-etsy-listing); also [Wealthy Tent](https://wealthytent.com/digital-products-9)
- "My Smooth Life" (opened March 2023) is reported at about **$32,000/month** from third-party Etsy analytics. — via [ProvenStartups / related search results](https://provenstartups.com/projects/goodnotes-digital-planner-etsy-listing) (aggregated in search results; the exact source page could not be verified)
- Amma Rose Designs reportedly made **$93,534** in Jan–Dec 2020, mostly from printable and fillable business planners. This comes from a wiki-style page and is outside the 2022–26 window. — [Grokipedia: Digital planners on Etsy](https://grokipedia.com/page/Digital_planners_on_Etsy)
- One analysis says almost every top-performing planner product it found was built for GoodNotes. — [Anlyzo: sell digital planners GoodNotes](https://anlyzo.com/blog/sell-digital-planners-goodnotes)
- Planner income ranges given by a guide: $200–$2,000/month for beginners (0–6 months) and $10,000–$50,000+ for advanced sellers (18+ months). These are unsourced. One example given was 250 downloads at $8.99, or about $2,247/month. — [Anlyzo](https://anlyzo.com/blog/sell-digital-planners-goodnotes); [Elite Wealth Plan](https://elitewealthplan.com/etsy-digital-downloads/)
- The spreadsheet shop **SimplyOrganizedPro** is estimated at about **$160K revenue in 2 years**, with 37K+ sales across 24 products. Its best seller is a Monthly Budget Spreadsheet with about 8.6K sales at $1–$2.50. The revenue figure is a secondary-blog estimate. — [Goodreads blog repost: "$160K in 2 Years Selling Spreadsheet Templates"](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24510163-160k-in-2-years-selling-spreadsheet-templates)
- Guides list monthly budget spreadsheets (Excel/Google Sheets) among the best sellers and say spreadsheet templates have low–medium competition. — [Growing Your Craft 2026](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/most-popular-digital-products-on-etsy); [Outfy 2026](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)
- A resume shop is cited at $4,200/month. This is unsourced and anecdotal. — [InsightAgent: best-selling templates](https://www.insightagent.app/blog/best-selling-templates-etsy)

**Platform context**
- Etsy marketplace GMS fell 4% in 2025 to $10.5B. Etsy does not break out digital products. — [Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://investors.etsy.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001370637-26-000019/etsy-20251231.htm); [Etsy Q4/FY2025 results](https://investors.etsy.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/218/etsy-inc-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results)
- Etsy does not disclose category GMS share, sales or listing counts. — [Branvas Etsy statistics](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/etsy-statistics-sellers-buyers-gms-fees)

### Inferences
- In demand order (by proxy): digital planners > resume/CV templates ≈ Notion templates > Canva templates > spreadsheet templates. In proven revenue per shop, though, cheap high-volume budget spreadsheets and GoodNotes planners have the clearest case studies.
- The $894K listing and $32K/month shop are survivorship outliers, and typical shops make far less.

### Gaps
- No primary (Etsy or seller-dashboard) revenue data for any work or productivity category.
- I could not get EverBee, eRank, Alura, Marmalead or Sale Samurai published trend reports. Searches turned up only their tool documentation, not category reports.
- No reliable numbers for business branding kits, content calendars, project-management spreadsheets, small-business bookkeeping templates, ADHD planners or ebook templates specifically.

## 2. Which are rising vs. saturated (Notion, Google Sheets, AI products, iPad planners)?

### Takeaway
Digital planners and resume templates are mature and very high-competition, and there are signs that broad-term planner search fell in 2025–26. Notion templates are high-volume but crowded and convert poorly for many Etsy sellers. Spreadsheet templates are rated low-to-medium competition and look like the best evergreen opening. AI prompt bundles are everywhere and cheap, but I found no independent evidence of sales growth.

### Cited Findings
- **Planners:** RankHero shows a 55% drop in search volume for "digital planners" between May 2025 and Apr 2026, peaking around Jan 2026. — [RankHero: digital planners](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planners). This conflicts with Listify's +38% YoY for "digital planner". — [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)
- "digital planner pdf" search volume held steady May 2025–Apr 2026, with an estimated CPC of $0.51. — [RankHero: digital planner pdf](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-pdf)
- "digital planner goodnotes" is estimated at only about 320 monthly searches against 117,853 listings (difficulty 70/100). RankHero advises using it as a secondary tag. — [RankHero: digital planner goodnotes](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-goodnotes)
- "digital planner pages" has about 50 searches against 77,012 listings. — [RankHero: digital planner pages](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-pages)
- Google Trends via Accio: "digital planners" peaked in Jan 2025 (index 16), then fell to about 6–9 by mid-2025. Accio separately claims an Aug 2025 peak for "etsy digital planner". Accio pages read as auto-generated, so treat them with caution. — [Accio: digital Google calendars trend](https://www.accio.com/business/trend-of-digital-google-calendars); [Accio: Etsy digital planner sales trend](https://www.accio.com/business/etsy-digital-planner-sales-trend-statistics)
- Some shops kept selling 2025 planners strongly months into the year (2025). — [Nasdaq/GOBankingRates: 3 bestselling items on Etsy for 2025](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/3-bestselling-items-etsy-2025)
- **Notion:** One seller saw about 1,200 Etsy results for "Notion Templates" and sold only 2–3 per month despite good ranking. Their paid ads lost money, and they concluded Etsy may not be the right audience. Another seller who started in July 2024 had $0 for two months, then reached $250+ by September 2024. — [The Mindful Planner Substack](https://themindfulplanner.substack.com/p/how-i-made-250-selling-notion-templates)
- One guide says niche Notion templates still have room even though courses and ebooks are saturated. This is a marketing source. — [Fungies 2026](https://fungies.io/?p=40481)
- An Etsy Notion shop has 335 sales since opening in 2025. — [NotionMadeEasyStudio on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/NotionMadeEasyStudio)
- **Spreadsheets:** Rated low–medium competition. — [Outfy 2026](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/); [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/most-popular-digital-products-on-etsy). Niche-business spreadsheets such as Etsy-seller bookkeeping (CSV import, fee categorization, dashboards) are a visible sub-niche. — [Etsy listing: Automated Etsy Bookkeeping](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4504735995/); [Monsterone](https://monsterone.com/google-sheets/etsy-seller-automatic-bookkeeping-in-excel-and-google-sheets-o548301/)
- **Resume/CV:** Rated "high" or "very high competition", averaging $5–$20. — [Listybox: resume templates](https://listybox.com/niche/resume-templates); [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/blog/best-selling-templates-etsy)
- **AI prompts:** Etsy prompt packs are priced around $5–$14.25, for example a 2026 pack of 150 prompts for Etsy sellers at $11.99. Gumroad bundles claim 27,000–50,000+ prompts, often with resale (MRR/PLR) rights. Income claims such as $3,000/month come from sellers of the bundles themselves. — [Etsy listing: 150 AI prompts](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4484537188/150-ai-prompts-for-etsy-sellers-2026); [Tipseason](https://tipseason.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-make-3-000-month-selling-ai-generated-digital-products-on-etsy)
- Etsy Community threads report steep sales drops for some sellers (e.g., an "Etsy sales down 70%" thread active mid-2025). This is anecdotal and not specific to digital. — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Digital-Prints/Decrease-on-sales-since-April-2021-Please-Help/td-p/134110172)

### Inferences
- **Saturated:** broad digital planners (GoodNotes/iPad), resume templates, generic Canva social templates, and AI prompt bundles (low barrier, PLR flooding, likely commoditized).
- **Steady or underserved:** Google Sheets/Excel business templates (bookkeeping, budgets, project trackers), plus app-specific niches where listing counts are low relative to intent.
- **Notion:** search demand exists, but Etsy buyers appear to convert worse than on Gumroad or Notion's own marketplace.

### Gaps
- No independent data on AI-prompt product sales trends on Etsy.
- Reddit r/EtsySellers threads were not surfaced by search, so there is no Reddit sentiment data.
- The tools' search-volume trend directions conflict (RankHero −55% vs. Listify +38%), and I could not resolve them.

## 3. Specific data on OneNote planners

### Takeaway
I found no third-party search-volume estimate for "OneNote planner". Etsy's own pages show an established niche: one OneNote shop has about 113.9K lifetime sales over about 7 years, and listings are positioned toward professional, work and project-management use. That positioning differs from the student and lifestyle angle typical of GoodNotes planners.

### Cited Findings
- One OneNote digital planner shop shows **113.9K sales** and about 7 years on Etsy. This is the only OneNote sales figure found. — [Etsy market: OneNote digital planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_digital_planner)
- Etsy lists the popular related searches as "onenote planner", "onenote work template", "onenote project management template" and "onenote weekly planner". — [Etsy market: OneNote](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote)
- OneNote listing prices run from about $2 to $39 and are often shown at 50% off. Bundles and student or professional editions sit at the high end. Numbers like "(7.3k)" beside listings appear to be review counts. — [Etsy market: OneNote digital planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_digital_planner)
- Example listings are business-oriented: "OneNote Template 2026 2027 Professional OneNote Planner for Project Management and Work Planning" and "OneNote Digital Planner for Professionals, One Note Template for WORK". — [Etsy listing 1603954210](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1603954210/onenote-planner-2025-2026-professional); [Etsy listing 799242534](https://www.etsy.com/listing/799242534/digital-planner-one-note-undated-with)
- There are separate OneNote sales and business sub-niches: "onenote sales planner" and "onenote template for sales". — [Etsy: onenote sales planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_sales_planner); [Etsy: onenote template for sales](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_template_for_sales)
- App-specific planner keywords generally have much lower volume than broad terms ("goodnotes" variant about 320 searches/month). — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-goodnotes)

### Inferences
- OneNote is a smaller but more work-focused niche than GoodNotes. Its buyers (Windows, Surface or corporate Microsoft 365 users) fit "work planner", "project management" and "sales planner" angles, which may make demand less tied to the January and back-to-school cycles than student planners. This is unverified.
- Because broad "digital planner" is saturated (about 700K–1M listings), long-tail keywords like "onenote work planner" or "onenote project management template" are probably the better ranking targets.

### Gaps
- No search volume, listing count or seasonality curve for "onenote planner" from any tool. Recommended next step: check eRank, EverBee or Alura directly, and Google Trends (5-year, US) for "onenote planner" vs. "goodnotes planner".
- I could not open the Etsy pages, so the listing count for "onenote planner" is unknown.

## 4. Typical price points, competition levels and seasonality

### Takeaway
Most work and productivity templates sell for under $20. Spreadsheets run $1–$15, resumes $5–$20, and Notion templates mostly under $10. Planners range from $2 to $39, with heavy permanent "sale" discounting. Seasonality is real for planners and budgets (January) and resumes (January, May–June). Business and resume templates are described as fairly steady year-round.

### Cited Findings
**Prices**
- Resume templates mostly sell for a few dollars up to about $13, e.g. $10.49 (down from $13.98), $2.81 (down from $11.22), even $0.80. Guides give $5–$20 or $5–$18. — [Etsy: resume template 2025](https://www.etsy.com/market/resume_template_2025); [Listybox](https://listybox.com/niche/resume-templates); [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/blog/best-selling-templates-etsy)
- Canva-editable resumes sell for $5–$15. — [Snazzy Designs Forever, seasonal digital products 2026](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- The best-selling budget spreadsheet sells for $1–$2.50. — [Goodreads repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24510163-160k-in-2-years-selling-spreadsheet-templates). A Payhip 10-tab accounting template sells for $1. — [Payhip](https://payhip.com/b/I9yYC)
- Notion templates are mostly under $10. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/notion-template)
- OneNote planners run $2–$39. — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_digital_planner)
- AI prompt packs on Etsy run $5–$14.25. Off-Etsy bundles run $29–$49. — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4484537188/150-ai-prompts-for-etsy-sellers-2026); [Tipseason](https://tipseason.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-sell-ai-prompts-even-as-a-beginner)

**Competition (listing counts as proxy)**
- "digital planner": about 694K listings (RankHero) or 1.04M (Listify). — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner); [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)
- "digital planner goodnotes": about 118K listings, difficulty 70/100. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-goodnotes)
- Resume templates: high or very high competition. Spreadsheets: low–medium. Notion: medium (difficulty 28/100). — sources above

**Seasonality**
- Resume templates peak in January (New Year job search) and May–June (graduation), with large volume year-round. — [Snazzy Designs Forever](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- January suits New Year planners, fitness trackers and budget templates. Budget trackers and meal planners hold steady regardless of season. — [Snazzy Designs Forever](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/); [MyDesigns 2026](https://mydesigns.io/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- Business templates, resume templates and social media graphics maintain steady year-round demand. — [LitCommerce 2026](https://litcommerce.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- Digital planner demand peaks in January (Listify), and "digital planners" peaked around Jan 2026 (RankHero). — [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planners)
- Google Trends advice: a 12-month window hides annual seasonality, so use a 5-year, region-filtered window. Trends is relative (0–100), not volume. — [Search Engine Land: SEO seasonality](https://searchengineland.com/guide/seo-seasonality); [Cited](https://cited.so/blog/google-trend)

### Inferences
- Planners show a January peak (well supported) and a back-to-school Aug–Sept bump (weakly supported). The only August evidence is Accio's "etsy digital planner" claim, and dated "2026–2027" academic listings imply an Aug–Sept academic-year cycle. Budget templates peak in January. Resumes peak in January and May–June. The supplied hypothesis of a September resume peak was **not confirmed**: sources say May–June instead.
- The low price points mean volume matters. Shops win on bundles, multi-app compatibility (Word, Google Docs, Canva, PDF) and permanent discounts.

### Gaps
- No month-by-month search curves for any keyword. Tool pages could not be fetched.
- No conversion-rate data by category. True CTR and conversion rates are not public.
