# Etsy Digital Product Demand by Month, January–September (2022–2026)

> Method note: My fetch tool could not resolve help.erank.com, koalanda.pro or snazzydesignsforever.com (DNS errors), so the eRank and Koalanda findings below come from search-result summaries of those pages, not from reading the full pages. Wherever possible, check them against the original page before quoting exact numbers. Etsy does not publish true sales or click-through data by product type. The proxies used here are eRank keyword rank and search counts, eRank's CTR metric (an estimate from its tool), review counts on Etsy listing pages, and seller-blog timing claims. I found no Google Trends or Pinterest Predicts data specific to digital products in the time available.

## What are the top-selling digital products per month/event on Etsy?

### Takeaway
Outside Q4, the main digital (instant-download) demand clusters are:
- January: New Year planners and budget printables.
- January to mid-February: Valentine's printables, especially classroom exchange cards.
- March–April: Easter (coloring pages, activity packs).
- Late April to June: Mother's Day, Father's Day and graduation, with printable cards and certificates.
- Wedding and party templates and games in spring and summer.
- July–August: back to school (teacher printables, classroom decor, "Meet the Teacher" templates, school-year calendars).

Ramadan/Eid printables form a crowded niche whose dates move each year. Ramadan begins roughly late Feb–early Mar in 2025–2026 (calendar fact, not from a source here).

### Inferences
- In 2025–2026, the clearest keyword-ranking spikes outside Q4 are Valentine's (January into February), Easter (March), Father's Day (May–June) and back to school (July–August). Graduation gift searches look weaker on Etsy in 2025 than in 2024.
- Mother's Day often shows up under general terms such as "gift for mom" rather than "mothers day". Sellers should target both.
- Classroom Valentines and teacher or back-to-school printables are the most clearly digital-native seasonal categories: cheap ($1–$4), high volume and parent- or teacher-driven.
- Planner and "digital download" terms are strong all year, with a January bump rather than a short spike.

### Gaps
- No verified Etsy search data for St. Patrick's Day, Pride, 4th of July, tax season or summer bucket lists or travel planners.
- No verified data on a Teacher Appreciation Week (early May) spike.
- No Google Trends curves or Pinterest Predicts digital-product items were retrieved.
- Years 2022–2023 are thinly covered; most keyword data is from 2025–2026.

## How early do searches rise before each event?

### Takeaway
Most sources agree that searches rise about 4–8 weeks before an event and that listings should be live 6–12 weeks ahead so they have time to rank. Valentine's searches ramp through January (Etsy starts promoting on site in early-to-mid January). Easter peaks in March. Back to school runs from July to a peak in late August.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy's Seller Handbook (2021) says Etsy begins promoting Valentine's items on site in early-to-mid January. — [Etsy Seller Handbook: Marketplace Insights Early 2021](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/marketplace-insights-early-2021-trends/1023216032625)
- Seller advice is to list Valentine's items by December, since sales start in early January and run to February 14. eRank advises getting "galentine gift" into listings more than six weeks before January. — [eRank Jan 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-january-2025/); [Snazzy Designs Forever](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- eRank's UK analysis says US Valentine shoppers on Etsy start earlier than UK shoppers. — [eRank UK Feb 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-february-2025)
- eRank recommends having Easter products live 8–12 weeks before the holiday. Specific product terms rise weeks before the broad "Easter" peak in March. — [eRank Easter 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/plan-for-easter-with-eranks-keyword-tool/)
- A MakerWords calendar gives these list-by targets:
  - Late November–early December for Valentine's.
  - January–February for spring holidays.
  - March for Mother's and Father's Day.
  - It advises working back from each peak and subtracting another 60–90 days for ranking.
  - — [MakerWords](https://makerwords.com/blog/holiday-products-to-sell-on-etsy)
- Mother's and Father's Day products start selling in late April and continue through June (seller guide). — [Snazzy Designs Forever](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- Back to school:
  - Shoppers start buying in July (2026 guide). — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/seasonal-selling-calendar)
  - One blog advises publishing teacher printables by May. — [InsightAgent: Trending Printables](https://www.insightagent.app/trends/trending-printables-on-etsy)
  - "School" search traffic peaked the week of August 28, 2025. — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-whats-trending-for-back-to-school-185685/)
- General guidance is to have listings live 6–8 weeks before peak demand, or create them 2–3 months ahead, and to keep seasonal listings active after the event so they keep their search history. — [InsightAgent calendar](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/seasonal-selling-calendar); [Lou PLR](https://www.louplr.com/blog/etsy-seasonal-selling-calendar)
- EverBee's seasonal guide (as quoted by ListifyAI) advises planning at least 4 months ahead and describes a 60–90-day evaluation window for new listings. Another source notes that Etsy does not actually take 8–12 weeks to index a listing, so this is a rule of thumb. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/when-to-list-on-etsy)
- Marmalead offers 30-day forward-looking trend graphs, while eRank's trend data is historical. A 2026 review warns that Marmalead has shown unexpectedly low volume for seasonal keywords at peak, so cross-check between tools. — [eComAIDaily](https://ecomaidaily.com/blog/erank-vs-marmalead-etsy-sellers-2026/); [Ecommerce Paradise](https://ecommerceparadise.com/marmalead-review-2026/)

### Inferences
Suggested listing lead times for digital products (my synthesis from the sources above):

| Event | Live by | Search ramp | Peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year / planners | November–December | — | Late December–January |
| Valentine's | December | January | Early February |
| St. Patrick's Day | Late January | — | — (no data) |
| Easter | 8–12 weeks before Easter | — | March (or early April when Easter is late) |
| Ramadan | 6–8 weeks before | — | Dates move about 11 days earlier each year |
| Mother's Day (US: 2nd Sunday of May) | March | Late April | Late April–early May |
| Father's Day | April | — | May–mid-June |
| Graduation | March–April | — | May–June |

### Gaps
- No primary week-level timing data for St. Patrick's Day, 4th of July, Pride, Eid or tax-season printables.
- Lead-time figures come mostly from seller and tool-vendor blogs, not Etsy data.

## Which events produce the biggest digital sales spikes?

### Takeaway
On keyword-rank proxies, Valentine's Day is the largest non-Q4 spike: "valentine downloads" was #1 in the UK in February 2026, and "valentines day gift" was #4 in the US in Q1 2026. Back to school (July–August) is second, as a sustained season, and Easter and Father's Day also break into eRank's top 20. Summer (June–July) is the overall slowest stretch, except for wedding. I found no direct sales-volume data by event; true CTR and sales are not public.

### Cited Findings
- "valentine downloads" was #1 on eRank's UK February 2026 list. — [eRank UK Feb 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-february-2026/)
- "valentines day gift" was #4 in US Q1 2026, up from #140. — [eRank Q1 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q1-2026/)
- "easter" was #14 in March 2025. — [eRank Mar 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-march-2025/)
- "fathers day" was #18 in May 2025 and in the June 2025 top 20. — [eRank May 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-may-2025/)
- "back to school" was a UK #15 "screamer" in July 2025, and eRank calls July and August big Etsy shopping months. — [eRank UK July 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-july-2025/)
- June and July run about 15% and 14% below average Etsy sales, with wedding the exception. January is a post-peak dip, not a strong month (Koalanda 2023). — [Koalanda 2023](https://koalanda.pro/blog/annual-etsy-sales-report-2023)
- The top overall Etsy search in Q2 2025 was "labubu", a physical fad, which shows that Q2 top-20 lists are mostly not digital. — [eRank Q2 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q2-2026/)
- Thank-you cards are described as having year-round demand; one listing reportedly has more than 306K sales. — [The Leap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/); [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)

### Inferences
- Likely ranking of non-Q4 digital spikes: 1) Valentine's (classroom cards, couples printables, "galentine"); 2) back to school (teacher printables, classroom decor, calendars and planners); 3) Easter (coloring pages, egg-hunt games, activity packs); 4) Mother's and Father's Day printable cards and certificates; 5) graduation, wedding and baby or bridal shower templates, which have steadier, life-event-driven demand.
- I found no evidence that Pride, 4th of July or St. Patrick's produce comparable digital spikes. Their absence from eRank top-20 summaries suggests they are smaller niches, but that is unverified.

### Gaps
- No public sales or conversion data by event; true CTR is not public, and eRank's CTR is a tool estimate.
- No r/EtsySellers threads were retrieved; the forum evidence is from the Etsy Community.
- Tax season (budget and tax organizer printables) and summer bucket list or kids' summer activity printables have no verified demand data in this research.
