# Keyword Demand and Competition: Budget Planner Keywords on Etsy and Google (2025-2026)

Note on sources: Live keyword-tool pages (RankHero) could not be fetched directly (DNS failure). Their figures below come from search-engine snippets of RankHero "Keyword Overview" pages dated Jan-Aug 2026. No public eRank, Marmalead, EverBee or Alura figures for these specific terms were found. All volume figures are third-party ESTIMATES. ## What search volume and competition figures exist for the core budget keywords?

### Takeaway
The best public 2026 data comes from RankHero's estimates. "budget planner" has the most volume (~60.5K/mo) but very heavy competition (~164K listings). "budget excel template" (~40.5K vs ~13K listings) and "debt snowball" (~27.1K vs ~4.6K listings) have the best volume-to-competition ratios. "budget spreadsheet" (~27.1K vs ~40K listings) is a strong mid-competition core term. No public data was found for "bill tracker", "debt payoff tracker", "no spend challenge", "paycheck budget", "finance planner", "money tracker" or "cash envelope".

## When does budget planner search peak, and what does that mean for listings published in Oct-Dec 2026 for 2027?

### Takeaway
Budget-related demand peaks in January. "budget planner" rose from ~60.5K to 74K in Jan 2026 (+22%) and dropped to 49.5K in February, and "cash stuffing" and "budget planer" also peaked in Jan 2026. Seasonality guidance says to list 4-8 weeks before the peak, so listings for 2027 should be live by mid-November 2026 at the latest, ideally in October.

### Cited Findings
- "budget planner": 60,500/mo from Apr to Dec 2025, then 74,000 in Jan 2026 and 49,500 in Feb 2026. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-planner)
- "budget planer" and "cash stuffing" both peaked in January 2026. "cash stuffing" fell from ~8,100 average to ~5,400 by Apr-May. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-planer); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/cash-stuffing)
- "expense tracker" and "food budget planner" were described as steady (Apr 2025-Mar 2026), which points to more evergreen demand. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/expense-tracker); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/food-budget-planner)
- ListifyAI builds its seasonality signals on 5 years of Google Trends data. It recommends listing ~6-8 weeks before the peak and promoting ~4 weeks ahead so listings are indexed and have reviews by then. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-seasonal-keywords)
- **ListifyAI January peaks**:
  - "printable planner" demand is highest in January and lowest in March; ListifyAI advises listing 4-6 weeks before January. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/printable-planner)
  - "digital planner" (1,044,640 listings) peaks in January. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)
- One planner guide claims December and January make up ~35% of annual planner sales and recommends stocking listings in October. This is an unverified vendor claim. — [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/sell-planners-on-etsy)
- A January sales guide says Etsy sales typically drop 40-60% from December peaks. It lists digital planners as "January-resistant" at +15 to +25%. This is a vendor estimate. — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-sales-january-post-holiday-slump)
- Google reported "saving money" as one of the top four New Year's resolution search trends for 2024. — [KSAT citing Google](https://ksat.com/news/local/2024/01/08/googles-top-new-years-resolution-trends-and-what-you-can-do-to-keep-yours-going)
- An older Think with Google UK report found savings-account searches peaked in Q1. — [Think with Google](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/1136/da552_TWG_UK_New_Year_Resolutions-01.pdf)

### Inferences
- The January lift for "budget planner" is moderate (~+22% over baseline), not a spike. Demand is largely evergreen with a New Year bump, so listings keep earning after January.
- To build sales history and reviews before the January 2027 peak, publish 2027-dated listings in October to early November 2026. Add "2027" variants as tags or title terms ("2027 budget planner" is 19 characters). Year-dated terms likely lose value after Q1.
- Feature-specific terms such as expense tracker and spending tracker look steadier and support sales outside January.

### Gaps
- No direct Google Trends curve for "budget planner", "budget spreadsheet" or "budget template" was found in published articles.
- No Etsy-native seasonality data for budget planners exists publicly.

## Which long-tail terms are trending upward in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
Little public evidence of upward trends was found. Most budget terms with trend data were flat or declining year over year:
- "weekly budget" ~1,300 → ~880
- "biweekly budget" -19%
- "debt snowball" -18%
- "budget planer" -21%

Cash stuffing remains culturally strong, with growing TikTok views, but its search volume spiked in January and then fell. Steady terms include expense tracker and food budget planner.

### Cited Findings
- "weekly budget" fell from ~1,300 in mid-2025 to ~880 in Apr 2026. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/weekly-budget)
- "biweekly budget" fell 19% from Apr 2025 to Mar 2026. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/biweekly-budget)
- "debt snowball" fell 18% from Jun 2025 to May 2026. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/debt-snowball)
- "budget planer" fell 21% from Jun 2025 to May 2026. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-planer)
- "expense tracker" and "food budget planner" held steady. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/expense-tracker); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/food-budget-planner)
- The #cashstuffing hashtag passed 3.5 billion views by early 2026, per SoFi (Apr 15, 2026). Earlier 2023 coverage cited ~1.3B, though the sources and methods differ. — [SoFi](https://www.sofi.com/learn/content/cash-stuffing/); [Good Morning America](https://ws.goodmorningamerica.com/shop/story/viral-cash-stuffing-trend-encourages-people-save-money-84542297)
- Competing products often bundle "Paycheck Budget Planner, Bill Tracker, Debt Payoff Tracker, Net Worth Tracker" in .xlsx form. Templates offer weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or paycheck cycles. — [Payhip listing](https://payhip.com/b/nDGi6); [Selar listing](https://selar.com/6dm51387m9)
- Competing listings stress Excel and Google Sheets compatibility, dashboards, auto-calculation and savings tracking. — [MonsterOne](https://monsterone.com/google-sheets/budget-planner-monthly-finance-tracker-excel-and-google-sheets-o548294/)
- RankHero suggests "budget planner" and "monthly budget" as supporting tags for "expense tracker". — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/expense-tracker)

### Inferences
- The year-over-year softness probably reflects normal fluctuation in Google-derived estimates and not collapsing demand. Still, there is no evidence that "weekly budget" is a rising term. Its value for this product lies in exact feature match and low volume-based competition, not growth.
- Paycheck-based budgeting is a common feature in competing products, which suggests buyer demand, but it is unmeasured as a search term. "paycheck budget" and "budget by paycheck" are worth testing as tags.
- Combining "weekly" or "bi weekly" with the high-ratio spreadsheet terms ("weekly budget" + "budget spreadsheet") likely gives a better long-tail position than "weekly budget planner" alone, which is unmeasured.

### Gaps
- No source reported terms that are rising in 2025-2026 specifically within the budget niche (for example, Etsy "trending searches", or eRank Trend Buzz entries for budget).
- No year-over-year data was found for "no spend challenge", "paycheck budget", "zero based budget" or "sinking funds".
