# Etsy and Google Keywords for an Editable Christmas Party Invitation Template (2025-2026 season)

Product: a digital Christmas party invitation template, editable in Canva, 5x7, red/green/gold, with an elegant Christmas tree design, sold as an instant download.

Research date: 2026-10-16. Method note: WebFetch could not reach etsy.com or rankhero.com (DNS failure), so every finding comes from web-search result summaries of the cited pages. These notes **add to** `research_notes/Etsy Christmas invitation listing images/alt_text_seo.md` and do not repeat the market pages, competitor titles or tag list already recorded there. Numbers are third-party estimates. RankHero is a vendor tool, not Etsy data.

## Search volume and competition figures (eRank, EverBee, Marmalead, Google Trends, keyword tools)

### Takeaway
The only phrase-level figures found are RankHero's Etsy estimates. "holiday party invite" gets about 2,400 searches a month on average, against 71,121 listings and a difficulty of 52/100. Its searches peak at about 9,900 in November and fall to about 210 by April. No public figures were found for "christmas party invitation" itself, or from eRank, EverBee or Marmalead. No Google Trends comparison was found either.

### Cited Findings
- **"holiday party invite" (Etsy, RankHero):** about 2,400 average monthly searches, 71,121 listings, difficulty 52/100 ("moderate"). The monthly series peaked at 9,900 in November 2025 and fell to about 210 in April 2026. — [RankHero: holiday party invite](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/holiday-party-invite)
- **"party invitation":** about 40,500 searches a month, more than 2.6M listings, difficulty 58/100. — [RankHero: party invitation](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/party-invitation)
- **"invitation":** about 550,000 searches a month, about 2.5M listings. — [RankHero: invitation](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/invitation)
- **"invitations":** 165,000 searches a month, 2,475,608 listings. — [RankHero: invitations](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/invitations)
- **"invitation template":** 33,100 searches a month, difficulty 60/100. Prices run from $1.49 to $16, with a median of $5 and an average of $5.59. — [RankHero: invitation template](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/invitation-template)
- **"digital invitation":** 9,900 searches a month. Prices run from $2.23 to $34.99, typically around $6.54. — [RankHero: digital invitation](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-invitation)
- **"christmas pajama":** about 110,000 searches a month (550,000 in December 2025), about 45,766 listings, difficulty 36/100. **"pajama party invite":** about 5,400 searches a month, 7,661 listings, difficulty 31/100. These cover a different design niche and are included only for comparison. — [RankHero: christmas pajama](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/christmas-pajama); [RankHero: pajama party invite](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/pajama-party-invite)
- **Caveat:** no outside tool has Etsy's real search volume, so every "Etsy search volume" figure in a paid tool is an estimate. — [Listify AI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/most-searched-keywords-on-etsy-2026)
- **Google:** Think with Google reports that holiday-season searches include "christmas party" and "christmas dinner". It gives no invitation-specific figures. — [Think with Google](https://thinkwithgoogle.com/consumer-insights/consumer-trends/holiday-searches-insights)

### Inferences
- "holiday party invite" peaks at about 4x its average in November. Christmas invitation terms probably follow the same September-to-December curve. Marmalead's timing in the earlier notes, with a climb from September and a late-November peak, agrees.
- The broad terms ("invitation", "party invitation") have more than 2M listings, so ranking for them alone is unrealistic. Combining "christmas party invitation" with "editable", "canva" and "template" is the realistic target.
- "holiday party invite" has about 30 listings per monthly search on average. The ratio improves a lot during the November peak.

### Gaps
- No figures for "christmas party invitation", "christmas invitation", "xmas party invite", "christmas party invitation template", "canva christmas invitation" or "red green gold christmas invitation". The RankHero pages for these either don't exist or couldn't be reached.
- No eRank, EverBee, Marmalead or Google Keyword Planner figures. A Google Trends "christmas vs holiday" comparison was not found and needs a manual check.

## Which modifiers matter most for this niche?

### Takeaway
In 26 competing titles, the near-universal words are christmas, party, invitation, template, editable, canva and holiday. Second-tier words are digital, invite, download and instant. Few competitors use printable, evite, xmas, dinner, elegant, tree, red, gold, 5x7 or green. Those are cheap differentiators that match this product's design.
