## 1. First (thumbnail/cover) image

### Takeaway
Etsy's own August 2025 handbook says not to put text overlays, collages or multiple images on the first photo if you want it optimised for search. Some third-party educators say a short 3–5-word overlay (e.g., "Editable Template") can help a digital product, provided it is large, high-contrast and placed inside the crop-safe zone. The safest approach is a clean, centred, styled mockup of the invite. Test any overlay before committing to it.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy Seller Handbook, "The Anatomy of a Well-Crafted Etsy Listing" (Aug 26, 2025): the first image wins the click from search, and later images win the sale. To optimise the first image for search, "avoid text overlays, collages, or multi-image thumbnails." Listings can hold up to 20 photos. — [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014) (seen only as search snippets via [Listadum summary](https://www.listadum.com/blog/whats-new-in-etsys-guide-to-crafting-a-winning-listing))
- A secondary summary of the handbook says the first image should be "clean, high-resolution, and free of text overlays." An eRank PDF (Nov 2025) quotes the handbook as saying the first photo should be horizontal or square so the focal point survives thumbnail cropping. — [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/whats-new-in-etsys-guide-to-crafting-a-winning-listing); [eRank photo guidelines PDF](https://help.erank.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/photo-guidelines-for-etsy-listings.pdf)
- Third-party advice disagrees on overlays:
  - Short overlay text can help when it adds information the photo can't show.
  - Digital-product thumbnails should stay around 3–5 words.
  - Overlays should be avoided unless very large and high-contrast.
  - Growing Your Craft suggests subtle overlays to highlight a USP.
  — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-thumbnails/); [RatioReady](https://ratioready.com/guides/how-to-create-listing-thumbnails-that-convert); [Dylan Jahraus](https://dylanjahraus.com/etsy-listing-thumbnail-photos-how-to-optimize-for-more-sales/); [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/course-chapter/high-converting-etsy-listing-photos-video-guide)
- InsightAgent claims that text-heavy thumbnails lose 70% of mobile clicks. The claim is unverified and has no study behind it. — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-seo-for-digital-products)
- Crop and size:
  - Etsy crops the first photo differently depending on where it appears, so keep the product centred, leave margins, and keep text away from the edges.
  - The common target is 2000×2000 px. One source attributes a 635×635 px minimum to Etsy.
  - The mobile thumbnail is roughly 170×135 px.
  — [ListifyAI 2026](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-listing-photos-size-requirements-2026); [Icons8](https://icons8.com/blog/articles/etsy-image-size/); [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-seo-for-digital-products)
- Mockup kits sold to Etsy sellers ship separate "thumbnail templates (main images)" and include crop-guide overlays "so you can ensure your text and important elements stay inside." Common canvases are 2000×2000 or 2500×2500 px squares, with 3000×2000 landscape as an alternative. — [Etsy mockup kit 1652872641](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1652872641/etsy-shop-listing-templates-etsy-store); [Etsy listing 1619634053](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1619634053)
- A Canva mockup tutorial recommends a mostly white, simple background with no people. It also says to centre the main text and the device mockup so they survive Etsy's crop. — [All About Planners](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/how-to-make-product-mockup-images-in-canva-for-etsy-or-your-online-shop-step-by-step-tutorial)
- Titles of observed Christmas bestseller-style listings put the format claims ("Editable Canva Template", "Instant Download", "5x7", "Mobile Invite", "Print or Send Options") in the title. These claims are not necessarily in the image. Examples:
  - "Festive Christmas Party Invitation: Editable Canva Template (5x7)"
  - "Editable Christmas Party Invitation, Minimalist Navy Blue Canva Template, Instant Download, Print or Send Options"
  - "Cocktail Christmas Party Invitation Template: Editable Canva E-vite"
  — [Etsy 1339913782](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1339913782/festive-christmas-party-invitation); [Etsy 4399767609](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4399767609/editable-christmas-party-invitation); [Etsy 1802126732](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1802126732/cocktail-christmas-party-invitation)

### Inferences
- In practice, the invite niche's cover is usually a styled flat-lay of the 5x7 card or a phone-screen mockup for evites, often with a small "EDITABLE TEMPLATE" or "Edit in Canva" banner. These sellers trade some of Etsy's search-optimisation advice for an immediate signal that the item is a DIY digital template. Any banner should be one short line, high-contrast, and well inside the centre-safe area.
- Seasonal props suit the cover: greenery, ornaments, gold confetti, an envelope. They should support the card, which must stay the dominant, legible element at about 170 px wide.

## 2. Showing editing on phone and laptop (device mockups)

### Takeaway
Sellers put device mockups in the image set because buyers need to know where they can edit. Listings disagree on phone editing: some say any device works, some say phone editing isn't available, and some say it works but a computer is recommended. The device image should state the seller's actual policy, and Corjl/Templett sellers pair it with a "Try the free demo" message.

### Cited Findings
- Canva invite listings vary on phone editing:
  - "edit your invitation template in Canva using your phone, iPad, tablet, or computer"
  - "phone/tablet editing is not available"
  - "basic mobile editing, computer recommended"
  — [Etsy 490489904](https://www.etsy.com/listing/490489904/swimming-pool-party-5x7-and-mobile); search-snippet sample of Etsy/Gumroad invite listings ([Etsy 4439756278](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4439756278), [Etsy AU 1347567693](https://www.etsy.com/au/listing/1347567693/modern-wedding-invitation-cute-wedding))
- Listing mockup kits for digital sellers include scenes named "Editable in Canva", "How it works", "Before & after", "What's included", "Product features", "Product benefits" and "Product description". These are the standard slide types. — [Etsy 1619634053](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1619634053); [Gumroad Etsy listing mockups](https://elizzastudio.gumroad.com/l/EtsyProductListingMockupTemplates)
- Canva mockup tutorial: a centred laptop mockup on a white background is a staple of digital-product listing photos. — [All About Planners](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/how-to-make-product-mockup-images-in-canva-for-etsy-or-your-online-shop-step-by-step-tutorial)
- Corjl and Templett listings standardly offer "TRY BEFORE YOU BUY! Copy and paste the URL below into your browser to demo the template." Most of one seller's listings carry a free demo link. Sellers also warn about limits:
  - "Demo edits will not be saved onto the purchased template."
  - "Most of the text is editable, please check the demo."
  - "Graphic elements are not editable and cannot be scaled, rotated or moved."
  - "Do not log in with Apple ID."
  — [Etsy 1439346515](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1439346515); [FarmhouseInvitations](https://www.etsy.com/shop/FarmhouseInvitations); [CelebratelyInvites](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CelebratelyInvites); [Etsy 1130540941](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1130540941)

### Inferences
- A typical device slide shows the invite open in the Canva editor on a laptop, with a phone beside it showing the same design. Common captions: "Edit in Canva on desktop or mobile", "Change text, fonts and colours", "No software to download".
- For Corjl/Templett, the device slide usually says "Edit in your browser – no software needed" and "Try the FREE DEMO before you buy (link in description)".
- If phone editing is restricted, put it on the image itself, e.g., "Desktop recommended". That reduces negative reviews. This is a reasoned inference from how often the listings state the restriction.

## 3. "What's included", "How it works / 3 steps", "What you can edit", "Benefits / why buy" images

### Takeaway
After the hero, the strongest supporting slides are informational graphics: what's included, how it works, what's editable, and FAQ. Etsy itself credits a seller who added a slide stating plainly that the item is a download. The copy points are consistent across listings: instant download, free Canva account (no Pro), 5x7 print plus a phone-size evite, all text editable, print at home or send by text, and a PDF with the template link.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy Seller Handbook (digital products article): one seller adds a fifth photo that explicitly states the listing is for a downloadable pattern, so shoppers understand what they're buying. — [Etsy Seller Handbook 47330319230](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/47330319230)
- Growing Your Craft calls these "informational listing photos" (feature lists, detail slides) and argues they raise conversion by reducing buyer uncertainty. — [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/how-to-boost-your-etsy-listing-conversion-rate-and-sales-with-informational-photos)
- Educator guidance for digital products recommends three things. Use a mockup hero, a detail close-up, and a "what's included" image. End with an instant-download call-to-action image and a text image listing the file formats. Put all product info in both the photos and the description. — [Listify/InsightAgent summary via search](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-listing-photos-digital-downloads); [Lilys.ai summary of a 2025 Canva-template tutorial](https://lilys.ai/en/notes/sell-digital-products-20251203/sell-canva-templates-etsy-beginner)
- Copy points in Canva invite listings (these are the claims sellers put on the "included" and "benefits" slides):
  - 5x7 print size, plus a phone-sized 1080×1920 px version "great for phone invites" (some bundle a 5x7 "great for emailing" with a phone-size file)
  - A PDF containing the Canva link
  - An optional back side or front + back
  - "Edit using a free Canva account" / "no Canva Pro required"
  - Instant downloads are non-returnable
  — [Etsy 490489904](https://www.etsy.com/listing/490489904/swimming-pool-party-5x7-and-mobile); [Gumroad sample](https://luminastudiolab.gumroad.com/l/DreamyBirthdayTemplates); [Gumroad sample](https://barecanvasph.gumroad.com/l/WeddingInvitation00A)
- Christmas-specific listings:
  - Cocktail Christmas e-vite ($4.99): "instant download… edit yourself in the free version of Canva… editable 5x7… fully edit all text"
  - Christmas Eve mobile invite: "sent digitally via mobile phone, or the 5x7 printed and mailed"
  - Gold wreath invite: "text, fonts, and colors can be customized… digital sharing or high-quality printing"
  - Phone evites priced at about $4.10–$6.00
  — [Etsy 1802126732](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1802126732/cocktail-christmas-party-invitation); [Etsy 1592872172](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1592872172/editable-christmas-eve-party-invitation); [Etsy 1835927739](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1835927739/christmas-party-invitation-canva); [Etsy 1795660004](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1795660004/digital-womens-christmas-party)
- "How it works" step pattern in seller templates: (1) purchase and download from Etsy, (2) open the PDF/link, (3) customise in Canva, (4) download and print or share. Canva templates need a free Canva account. — [Etsy 1619634053](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1619634053)
- Animated or video evites are a distinct sub-format, e.g., "Pink and Gold Christmas Party Video Invitation: Editable Canva Template". — [Etsy 4395750136](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4395750136/pink-and-gold-christmas-party-video)
- Some shops upsell an "edit for me" service with 24–48 hour turnaround. This can go on a benefits slide. — [Etsy mamaChrisDesign](https://www.etsy.com/shop/mamaChrisDesign)
- Licensing lines ("personal use only, no resale of editable files") commonly appear. — [Gumroad Christmas bundle](https://digivaultbyateeq.gumroad.com/l/Invitation)

### Inferences: the typical 7–10 image sequence (reconstructed)
1. **Hero:** styled flat-lay of the 5x7 invite with Christmas props. At most an optional one-line "EDITABLE TEMPLATE" banner.
2. **Second design view:** the phone or evite version on a smartphone screen ("Send by text or email"). Alternatively, a print plus phone side-by-side ("Print or send digitally").
3. **Editor/device slide:** laptop and phone showing the Canva (or Corjl) editor. Caption: "Edit in Canva – free account", "Desktop & mobile" (or "Desktop recommended"). For Corjl/Templett: "Try the free demo".
4. **What you can edit:** before/after or callout arrows ("Edit all text", "Change fonts & colours", "Add photo"). List the non-editable elements too.
5. **What's included:** icons for the 5x7 (and 4x6 or A5 if offered), the 1080×1920 phone version, the PDF with the template link, and the optional back side.
6. **How it works, 3–4 steps:** Purchase → Open link → Edit → Download (print at home or print shop / text, email or social).
7. **Benefits / why buy:** Instant download, no shipping, unlimited edits, print as many as you need, free Canva, message-for-help support.
8. **Matching suite / cross-sell:** coordinating menu, sign or thank-you card with the same design.
9. **Detail close-up / alternate colourway.**
10. **FAQ / "this is a digital item, nothing will be shipped" disclaimer.** This mirrors the Etsy-endorsed "explicitly state it's a download" slide.
- A 3–15 s listing video of the Canva editing flow goes in the video slot (see section 5).

- I could not confirm the exact slide order of any specific bestseller, since page images were inaccessible. Treat the order above as a synthesis.
- "Unlimited edits" and "print as many as you like" are common seller phrases, but I found no snippet that sources them directly.

## 4. Colour, typography, branding consistency and thumbnail legibility

### Takeaway
Use one template system across every slide: the same background, palette, fonts, logo placement and safe margins. Pull colours from the invite itself, keep slide text to a few large words, and design for a thumbnail about 170 px wide.

### Cited Findings
- Sellers buy Canva listing-template kits to keep visuals uniform. One kit pairs 40+ thumbnail templates with 150+ listing mockups, editable with a free Canva account. — [Etsy 1652872641](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1652872641/etsy-shop-listing-templates-etsy-store); [Etsy 1743217099](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1743217099/colorful-etsy-shop-listing-templates-190)
- A Canva Brand Kit keeps logos, colours and fonts in one place for listing images, mockups and inserts. Templates let you swap colours or text without rebuilding the image. — [Brenda Cadman](https://brendacadman.com/how-etsy-sellers-can-use-canva/); [Medium/SpySeller](https://medium.com/@spyseller/how-to-create-on-brand-etsy-listing-graphics-without-overdesigning-fc2a00fa1adf)
- Listing photos for digital products must be "simple and easy to read" and show exactly what the buyer gets. — [Lilys.ai tutorial summary](https://lilys.ai/en/notes/sell-digital-products-20251203/sell-canva-templates-etsy-beginner)
- eRank says mockups must accurately represent what you sell, use lifestyle context and stay uncluttered, and warns against misleading AI-generated mockups. — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/mockups-for-your-print-on-demand-etsy-listings/)
- Thumbnail legibility: about 3–5 words, high contrast, text away from the edges, readable at the roughly 170×135 px mobile size. — [RatioReady](https://ratioready.com/guides/how-to-create-listing-thumbnails-that-convert); [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-seo-for-digital-products)

### Inferences
- For Christmas, a restrained palette pulled from the design (e.g., deep green, red or burgundy, gold, cream) works well. Use one display font for headings, plus one clean sans-serif at large weight for callouts.
- Avoid putting thin script fonts in the banner text, since they become illegible at thumbnail size.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative study on font size or contrast thresholds for Etsy thumbnails.

## 5. Data on image order and types for conversion

### Takeaway
Hard data is scarce. The only first-party statistic found is that Etsy's July 2020 analysis of over 5 million buyers showed buyers were more likely to purchase from listings with video. Everything else is directional educator advice, or unverified figures from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy (community / Etsy Insights): "buyers are more likely to purchase an item with a listing that has video," based on a July 2020 analysis of over five million buyers. Etsy also advised adding video to the highest-traffic listings first. — [Etsy Community: Etsy Insights video tips](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-video-tips-166074/)
- The claim that video listings get "2x orders" circulates on tool blogs, but no original Etsy source was found, so it is unverified. — [Splento](https://splento.com/blog/photography/etsys-video-features)
- Sources disagree on the video spec: 5–15 s or 3–15 s, up to 100 MB. — [Splento](https://splento.com/blog/photography/etsys-video-features); [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-listing-photos-size-requirements-2026)
- Etsy handbook: the first image drives the search click, and subsequent images drive the sale. — [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014)
- The claim that "mockup listings convert 40% better" comes from a single source with no study. Do not rely on it. — [Gerald](https://joingerald.com/learn/work--income/sell-photos-etsy-guide)
- Educators recommend A/B testing the cover, with and without overlay text, and tracking search click-through rate and favourites in Etsy Stats. — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-thumbnails/)
- A 2026 Outfy roundup lists listing photo and video templates themselves among top-selling Etsy digital products. This shows how much sellers prioritise these images, but it is not conversion data. — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)

### Inferences
- Since the cover drives clicks and the later slides drive purchase, a sensible approach has two parts. Optimise the cover for search CTR with a clean mockup, then front-load slides 2–5 with the information that removes objections: device or editability, what's included, and how it works. Add a short editing-demo video.

### Gaps
- I found no Etsy-published or third-party controlled data on optimal slide order, the ideal number of photos, or the conversion lift from specific informational slides for invitation templates.
- I found no Everbee, eRank or Marmalead study quantifying image effects for this niche.
