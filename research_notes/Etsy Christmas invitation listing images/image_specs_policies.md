# Etsy Listing Image Specs and Policies for Square (1:1) Digital-Template Listings (Canva Christmas Invitations)

Method note (2026-10-16): Direct page fetches of help.etsy.com, etsy.com, community.etsy.com and canva.com were blocked by this session's network proxy (DNS/CONNECT refused). All findings below come from search-engine extracts of those official pages, plus third-party seller guides where noted. Exact wording should be spot-checked in a browser before quoting verbatim. Several Etsy help pages contradict each other, and those conflicts are flagged below.

## 1. Image size, resolution, aspect ratio, file types, file size, photo and video counts

### Takeaway
Etsy's official guidance is at least 2000 px on the shortest side, at 72 PPI. Keep each file under about 1 MB. JPG, PNG, GIF or HEIC are accepted, but not animated GIF or transparent PNG. Up to 20 photos and up to 2 videos are allowed per listing. A square 2000x2000 (or 3000x3000) JPG is safe and within spec. The old "10 photos" limit is outdated.

### Cited Findings
- Recommended size: shortest side 2000 px at 72 PPI. The Seller Handbook repeats the 2000 px shortest-side figure. — [Etsy Help: Requirements and Best Practices for Images (UK, art. 208)](https://www.etsy.com/uk/help/article/208); [Etsy Seller Handbook: Creating Listings That Convert](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/create-listings-that-convert/366469719354)
- The US version of the help article says width and height should each be at least 2000 px. The first photo should be at least 635 px wide and tall, or it may show up lower in search. — [Etsy Help: Requirements and Best Practices for Images](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop)
- File size: images over 1 MB "may not finish uploading," especially on slow connections. An older Etsy FAQ cited 300 KB. Treat the 1 MB figure as a recommendation, not a hard cap. — [Etsy Help art. 208](https://www.etsy.com/uk/help/article/208); [Etsy Help: Requirements and Best Practices](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop)
- Accepted formats are JPG, GIF, PNG and HEIC (some extracts also list SVG). Animated GIFs and transparent PNGs are not supported, and transparent areas render black. — [Etsy Help: Requirements and Best Practices](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop); [Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)
- Photo count: up to 20 photos per listing. — [Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)
- Video specs: maximum file size 100 MB. Formats are MP4, MOV, FLV, AAC, AVI, 3GP and MPEG. Minimum resolution is 500 px and the ideal is at least 1080 px. Videos play without audio, and anything over 15 s is clipped. — [Etsy Help: How to Add Listing Videos](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053206073-How-to-Add-Listing-Videos); [Etsy Seller Handbook: Listing videos with your smartphone](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-to-take-listing-videos-with-your/821069114343)
- Conflict on video length: one page says 5–15 s and another says 3–15 s. — same sources as above
- Conflict on video count: the listing guide says 2 videos, while another help page says 1 video per listing. — [Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing); [Etsy Help: How to Add a Listing Video (IN)](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/360053206073-How-to-Add-a-Listing-Video?segment=selling)
- Third-party consensus: 2000x2000 (1:1) or 2700x2025 (4:3) are common safe sizes, kept under about 1 MB. Etsy recompresses uploads, so very large files (for example 8000 px) add no visible benefit. — [LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size/); [Outfy 2026 guide](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size-guide/)
- Outlier: Closo recommends 2400x2400 based on its own tests. No other source corroborates this. — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/optimization-growth-strategies/how-to-etsy-listing-photo-size-2025-what-actually-works-after-testing-thousands-of-images)

### Inferences
- For a square digital-template listing, export 2000x2000 px (or 2400–3000 px for extra zoom headroom) as sRGB JPG at roughly 80–85% quality, aiming for 1 MB or less. Avoid transparent PNG.
- Use more than the old 10-image habit allows. Up to 20 photos plus a short (5–15 s), silent, captioned video of the template being edited in Canva is within spec. Text overlays carry the message because video audio never plays.

### Gaps
- No current Etsy page fetched directly confirmed whether 1 MB is a hard limit. The real upload ceiling is believed to be higher, but no source confirmed it.
- The video count (1 vs 2) and minimum length (3 vs 5 s) conflict across Etsy pages. Verify in Listing Manager.
- Etsy's video help states a "2:1 or 1:2" aspect ratio range, but no source confirmed whether square video is cropped.

## 2. Thumbnail cropping (search, shop grid, mobile app) and square safe zones

### Takeaway
Etsy's own help pages are internally inconsistent. One section says the first photo should be landscape or square so the centre survives crops. Another says to avoid square crops and prefer horizontal/4:3. Third-party 2025–2026 guides report desktop search moving to square or near-square (1:1 or 5:4) thumbnails, with mobile close to square. A square image with all critical content in the central area is the robust choice. Etsy now offers a thumbnail preview and an "Adjust Thumbnail" tool.

### Cited Findings
- Help-page guidance on the first photo: "The first photo in a listing should be horizontal (landscape) or square. This ensures that the center focal point of the image appears in the cropped thumbnail views." — [Etsy Help: Requirements and Best Practices](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop?segment=selling)
- The same article family also recommends a 4:3 ratio and advises against square crops. It says to shoot horizontally because vertical images lose more when cropped. — [Etsy Help art. 208](https://www.etsy.com/uk/help/article/208)
- Thumbnail tool: in Shop Manager, go to Listings, edit the listing and choose "Adjust Thumbnail." Position the subject so it is clear in all three views Etsy uses. Crop before adjusting the thumbnail. The first photo is the thumbnail. — [Etsy Help: How to Edit Your Listing Photos](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016260113-How-to-Edit-Your-Listing-Photos)
- Etsy's update log says sellers can now preview listing thumbnails before publishing. — [Etsy Help: Newly Crafted – Etsy Updates for Your Shop](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/10603291042967-Newly-Crafted-Etsy-Updates-for-Your-Shop)
- Etsy previously announced changes to listing image size and ratio (exact date not retrievable). — [Etsy Community Announcement: "We're making updates to size and ratio for listing images"](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Announcements/We-re-making-updates-to-size-and-ratio-for-listing-images/td-p/142858294/)
- Third-party reports conflict. Some say desktop search moved to square 1:1 thumbnails. Others report roughly 5:4 on desktop, tighter square on mobile, or 4:5 on desktop. — [Outfy 2026](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size-guide/); [Growing Your Craft (2026)](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-optimal-size-aspect-ratio); [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-thumbnails/)
- Digital listings get a "Digital Download" label under the listing in search, and the Etsy app shows "Instant Download" on the listing image. — [Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)

### Inferences
- Practical safe zone for a 2000x2000 square: keep the headline, the invitation card and any badge inside the central area (roughly the middle 80% horizontally and vertically). That survives a 4:3 crop, which trims about 12.5% top and bottom, and a 5:4 or 4:5 crop, which trims about 10% on one axis. Keep about 250 px of margin from every edge clear of text.
- Avoid putting a corner badge (for example "Edit in Canva") in the top-left or bottom-left corner. The app's "Instant Download" overlay may sit on the image, and crops eat the corners. Place badges inset from edges.
- Make text large. Search thumbnails display at roughly 170–300 px wide, so headline text should stay readable at about 10% scale.
- Always verify with Etsy's thumbnail preview and Adjust Thumbnail tool after upload.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 Etsy document stating the exact current thumbnail aspect ratio per surface (desktop search, shop grid, app) could be retrieved. The 1:1 vs 4:3 vs 5:4 conflict is unresolved, so verify with the live preview.
- The exact placement of the "Instant Download" overlay in the app could not be confirmed.

## 3. Etsy policies relevant to digital template listings (mockups, Canva trademark, Creativity Standards, digital disclosure)

### Takeaway
Digital templates must be designed by the seller. Reselling a pre-existing design or an unmodified Canva or manufacturer template is not allowed. Photos must be the seller's own and must not misrepresent the item. Rendered mockups of the seller's own design are the normal way to show a digital product, but stock or other sellers' images are not. Canva's trademark guidelines restrict use of the Canva logo in sales materials. Factual mention of the word "Canva" (for example "editable in Canva") is common practice, but no explicit Etsy or Canva permission for it was found. Etsy flags the listing as Digital Download automatically, but sellers should also state clearly that no physical item ships.

### Cited Findings
- Creativity Standards: every listing must be made, designed, handpicked or sourced by the seller. Digital downloads must be the seller's original content. "A bundle, collection, scan, or PDF of someone else's work" does not qualify. — [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity)
- "Digital items listed in your store must be made and/or designed by you, the seller." They can be premade files or files custom-made after purchase. — [Etsy Seller Handbook: How Etsy Is Protecting Creativity](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276491338090)
- Reselling a pre-existing design, or choosing from a manufacturer's templates, does not count as "designed by" the seller and is grounds for removal. — [Etsy Help: Reasons a Listing May Be Removed Under Creativity Standards](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/34707360607511-Reasons-a-Listing-May-Be-Removed-Under-Etsy-s-Creativity-Standards)
- AI: seller-prompted AI art is allowed but must be disclosed in the listing description. AI prompt bundles are prohibited. — [Etsy Seller Handbook: Etsy's stance on AI](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004)
- Photos: sellers must use their own photographs or video, "not stock photos, artistic renderings, or photos used by other sellers or sites." — [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
- Listing Image Requirements: photos should showcase the actual finished product. For custom items, the first image must show a finished, customized item. Placeholder mockups cannot be the main image, but computer-generated mockups may be used in additional images to show customization options. — [Etsy Listing Image Requirements](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005)
- Handbook: edits must not alter or misrepresent the product's appearance. Using a third-party production-partner mockup instead of showing the actual item is a common violation. — [Etsy Seller Handbook: 4 Policy Best Practices](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1075628311049); [Etsy Seller Handbook: Truthful Advertising](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1450964704111)
- IP: items that violate Etsy's intellectual property policies are prohibited, and rights holders can report them. — [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
- Canva trademark guidelines (older PDF): use of Canva trademarks may be prohibited unless expressly authorized. "Only Canva and its authorized licensees may use the Canva Logo in advertising, promotional, and sales materials." — [Guidelines for Using Canva Trademarks (PDF)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/static-cse.canva.com/pages/media-kit/Guidelines+for+Using+Canva+Trademarks.pdf)
- Digital listing mechanics: choose "Digital files" as the item type when creating the listing. Up to 5 files of 20 MB each. An instant-download listing cannot be published without at least one file. — [Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing); [Etsy Help: How to Manage Your Digital Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings)
- Etsy auto-labels digital items "Digital Download" in search and "Instant Download" in the app. — [Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)

### Inferences
- For a digital invitation, the "actual product" is the design itself. The main image should be a faithful render of the seller's own invitation, in a flat or simple card mockup, not a placeholder or another seller's photo. Mockups such as a phone screen, printed card on a table or Canva editor screenshot are appropriate in secondary images as long as they show the real design. Use only mockup scenes the seller has a licence for.
- Do not use the official Canva logo or wordmark artwork in thumbnails. Plain text such as "Edit in Canva" or "Editable Canva template" is a descriptive, referential use and is widespread, but it is still a mild trademark-risk area. Avoid styling it to look like Canva's logo, and avoid implying endorsement ("Official Canva").
- Put "DIGITAL DOWNLOAD – no physical item will be shipped" on at least one image (ideally the "what you get" image) and in the first lines of the description, in addition to Etsy's automatic label. This reduces "where's my card?" disputes.
- Build each invitation from scratch or substantially modify it. Do not list a lightly edited Canva-library template, which would violate both Etsy's Creativity Standards and Canva's licence.

### Gaps
- No Etsy page explicitly addressing mockups for purely digital products was found. The guidance above is inferred from the physical and custom-item rules.
- A current (2025–2026) Canva trademark or brand-usage page could not be retrieved. The PDF found may be dated, and no explicit Canva statement on sellers writing "Canva" in titles or images was found.
- Etsy's Intellectual Property Policy text on brand names in titles ("compatible with" wording) was not retrievable.

## 4. Canva buyer-editing requirements (free account, template-link PDF delivery) and what to state

### Takeaway
Canva allows selling templates as Canva template links. Templates containing Pro content may only be sold as links, never as PDFs or other files. Generating a template link requires a paid Canva plan for the seller. Buyers on free Canva accounts see Pro elements watermarked. To make the claim "edit with a free Canva account" true, build with Free elements and your own assets only. Deliver via a PDF (uploaded to Etsy as the digital file) containing the template link.

### Cited Findings
- Templates with Pro content can be used "only when shared as Canva template links." They "can't be sold as a PDF or other file types, whether flattened or not." Free-content-only templates (plus your own photos) can be sold as PDFs or links. Canva's example is an invitation-template seller. — [Canva Help: Use Canva to design products for sale](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/)
- Existing Canva templates cannot be resold as-is. Create from scratch or substantially modify text, fonts, colours and layout. Canva content cannot be sold standalone (clipart or stock as-is). — [Canva Help: Use Canva to design products for sale](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/); [Canva Content License Agreement](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/)
- Creating and sharing a template link is only available to Canva Pro, Teams, Enterprise, Education and Nonprofit users. — [Canva Help: Share your design as a template link](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/share-template-link/)
- Canva Free users see Pro content (templates, images) watermarked. Removal requires a one-off element purchase or a Pro upgrade. — [Canva Help: Watermarks on my design](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/watermarks-design/); [Canva Help: Premium elements](https://www.canva.com/help/premium-elements/)
- Conflict: Canva's older "Licensing explained" page is more restrictive on Pro content in templates. The newer Help Centre page supersedes it. — [Canva: Licensing explained](https://www.canva.com/licensing-explained/)
- Etsy digital file limits for the delivery PDF: up to 5 files of 20 MB each. — [Etsy Help: How to Manage Your Digital Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings)

### Inferences
- Recommended buyer-requirement statements for images and description:
  - "Instant digital download – NO physical item shipped"
  - "Edit in Canva with a FREE account" (only if every element is Free or seller-owned; otherwise say "some elements require Canva Pro")
  - "You'll receive a PDF with your template link" (plus how to access it: Etsy Purchases page or download email)
  - "Edit text, fonts, colours; download to print or send digitally" (list editable vs fixed elements)
  - "Works on desktop, or with the Canva mobile app" (if tested)
  - "Personal use only / not for resale" (licence line)
  - "Print at home or at a print shop" (state the size, for example 5x7 in, and bleed if relevant)
  - "Colours may vary by screen/printer"
  - "Digital item: no refunds/returns" (if that is the shop policy; Etsy lets sellers set this)
- Use a dedicated "How it works" image (1. Buy, 2. Download PDF, 3. Click link, 4. Edit in Canva, 5. Print/send) and a "What's included" image. These are standard in the category and address the main buyer confusions.
- Share the template link in "template link" mode, not as an edit link. An edit link would let buyers modify the master.

### Gaps
- The source text did not state whether Pro elements in a sold template link appear watermarked to a free-account buyer. The inference that they do is based on general watermark behaviour.
- Canva's guidance on how sellers may describe Canva compatibility in marketing was not found.
