## 1. Which themes and aesthetics are rising for holiday 2026 that can become digital products?

### Takeaway
Etsy's official Fall/Winter 2026 Trend Edit was published around Sept–Oct 2026 with Etsy's first Guest Trend Editor, Mandy Lee. It names seven trends: **Craftmas, Merrymaxxing, Operatic Drama, Family Reimagined, Mark of the Maker, Table Dressing and Freaky Shoes**. Craftmas (DIY gatherings) and Table Dressing (hosting) map most directly to printables and patterns. eRank's 2026 holiday post adds heirloom berry-red/green with Pantone 2026 "Cloud Dancer" white, frosty blues and silvers, glam gatherings, twisted woodland and an emerging "Tropical Christmas".

## 3. Etsy policy and platform changes (2025–2026) that make digital product types riskier or better

### Takeaway
The biggest 2026 change for digital sellers is Etsy's **digital-to-print test** (announced Sept 2026, US/Canada). Buyers of eligible digital files can order them printed on posters, mugs or apparel through FYUL's (Printify/Printful parent) "Ventur Editions" shop. **Sellers receive no extra pay for this, and the only opt-out is shop-wide.** Other changes are the AI disclosure requirement (Creativity Standards), a ban on AI prompt bundles, a revised Cases ("not as described") policy (9 July 2026), and DST-linked Regulatory Operating Fee increases (22 June 2026). Etsy also has a new CEO (Kruti Patel Goyal, 1 Jan 2026) and cut ~12% of its staff in Q2 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Digital-to-print test:** buyers of eligible digital downloads see an option to order a printed version (poster/mug/apparel) via a separate checkout. Eligibility: US/Canada shops that don't already offer a physical version or their own POD integration, and whose files meet the print partner's requirements. Opt-out is shop-wide only — [Etsy Help: Printed Items From Digital Purchases](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/43476182453399-How-Printed-Items-From-Digital-Purchases-Work-for-Sellers); [Etsy Community announcement](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Announcements/We-re-running-a-test-in-the-US-for-buyers-who-purchase-digital/m-p/140027782)
- FYUL runs the Ventur Editions shop. Creators get no compensation for printed versions, Etsy promotes the shop with pop-ups on other sellers' order pages, and Etsy declined to give the test size. This follows a 2025 test with Shutterfly and Printify (coverage ~Oct 2026) — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-fyul-pod-test/); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/etsy-confirms-printify-and-printful-parent-fyul-runs-its-new-digital-to-print-test-and-sellers-get-nothing-for-printed-designs/)
- **AI rules:** AI-made items are allowed but must be disclosed. The Creativity Standards (last updated 10 Jun 2025) carry the disclosure requirement, and items must be "based on a seller's original design". **AI prompt bundles are prohibited.** Secondary sources give conflicting enforcement dates (Jan 14 2026, Mar 1 2026, July 9 2026). A claim that "12,000 listings removed" in Q1 2026 is unverified — [Growtsy: Etsy AI Policy](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy); [PromptlessPress](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products); [Bulk Mockup: Policy changes 2026](https://www.bulkmockup.com/etsy-policy-changes/); [PR Creative Digital (unverified figure)](https://www.prcreativedigital.com/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026-shoppers.html)
- **Cases policy (effective 9 July 2026, per blog):** an item that matches its description but merely disappoints the buyer is not eligible for a "not as described" case. For digital files, the test is whether the download matches the listing — [Listify: digital not-as-described refunds](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-digital-not-as-described-refunds)
- **Fees:** on 22 June 2026 Etsy raised its Regulatory Operating Fee in markets with digital-services taxes; rates and countries were not found. Offsite Ads remain 15% (<$10K annual sales) or 12% (≥$10K). Sources conflict on whether ≥$10K shops can opt out — [iscompliant: Etsy policy updates 2026](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-policy-updates-2026); [Craftybase: Offsite Ads](https://craftybase.com/blog/everything-you-should-know-about-etsy-offsite-ads); [Fenfair](https://fenfair.com/blog/posts/etsy-offsite-ads-true-cost)
- **Search (third-party claims, unverified):** a 2026 shipping-cost ranking factor (> $6 US shipping penalized, which doesn't affect digital) and heavier weighting of CTR, add-to-cart and purchase completion relative to impressions — [ListingView: Etsy algorithm 2026](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-s-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026); [ListingLab](https://listinglab.pro/blog/etsy-algorithm-2026)
- **Corporate:** CEO Kruti Patel Goyal from 1 Jan 2026 — [Marmalead: Etsy policy updates](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-policy-updates/). Q2 2026: marketplace GMS ~$2.6B (+7.5% YoY, third straight growth quarter), revenue $668M, app GMS +12.5%. Etsy sold Depop to eBay for $1.4B, cut ~220 roles (~12%) and authorized a $2B buyback. Q3 GMS guide $2.53–2.58B (+4–6%) — [Etsy Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm); [MarketBeat Q2 call highlights](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/etsy-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-08/)

### Inferences
- **Riskier:** wall art, posters and quote/typography printables, which are now eligible for digital-to-print. Etsy's partner can capture the physical-print sale that a seller could otherwise have made; sellers must weigh the shop-wide opt-out. Also riskier: AI-generated clip-art/PNG bundles (disclosure plus original-design rules) and AI prompt packs (banned). Trademark/pop-culture items carry IP takedown risk.
- **Better or neutral:** functional digital products (OneNote/GoodNotes planners, spreadsheets, templates) are not print-eligible and are unaffected by the shipping ranking factor. The 9 July 2026 Cases change protects sellers whose listings accurately describe the file (compatibility, page count, what's included).
- Etsy's GMS growth has resumed (+7.5% in Q2 2026), and the app is growing faster than overall GMS, so mobile-legible thumbnails matter.

- Exact Regulatory Operating Fee rates and countries could not be retrieved.
- No official Etsy statement on how digital items are ranked or filtered in search in 2026 was found.
- No Reddit r/EtsySellers threads surfaced in searches, so seller-reported impacts of the print test are undocumented here.
- Q3 2026 earnings are not yet released; they are typically late Oct or early Nov.
