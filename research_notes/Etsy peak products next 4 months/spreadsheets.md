# Etsy Spreadsheet Templates (Excel / Google Sheets): Peak Products for Nov 2026 – Feb 2027

**Method and limits (read first):**
- Research date: 21 Oct 2026.
- Direct page fetching was blocked in this session. The egress proxy refused rankhero.com, etsy.com and help.erank.com, and WebFetch could not resolve DNS. Every number below therefore comes from **search-engine snippets** of RankHero keyword pages, Etsy listing/market pages and interview articles. I did not view the pages themselves.
- RankHero "search volume" is a Google-style volume estimate tied to Etsy keywords. It is **not** Etsy's internal search count. Its competition figures are often inconsistent within a single page (two different listing counts). Treat every volume, competition and difficulty number as a **proxy**.
- True Etsy CTR is not public. No eRank CTR figures for spreadsheet keywords surfaced.
- Prior notes in `research_notes/Etsy digital products seasonal demand/` already contain two findings. The SimplyOrganizedPro ~$160K/2 yrs case and the "spreadsheets = low–medium competition" claim are not repeated here except as a cross-reference.

## Q1. Which spreadsheet keywords spike in Dec–Jan, and what are the specific products to sell (15+ list)?

### Takeaway
The only hard monthly data available is from RankHero, and it confirms a **January peak** for core budget terms. "Budget tracker spreadsheet" was 1,300 searches in Jan 2026 versus about 880 in other months, a lift of roughly +48%. "Tax tracker" peaks in **February**, at 4,400 searches in Jan 2026 with the peak in Feb. The broad "budget template google sheets" term is large, at about 40.5K/mo, but fell 33% between Aug 2025 and Jul 2026. A successful seller describes January as the biggest month in the budgeting niche, and the month when the highest-priced annual budget sheets sell.

### Inferences
- January is the dominant spreadsheet month. The single verified monthly series ("budget tracker spreadsheet") shows January about 48% above baseline, and the leading seller confirms it anecdotally. A December lift was **not** visible in that series: Dec 2025 sat at the ~880 baseline. Listings should be live and indexed by **late November to early December** so they have reviews and ranking before the January wave.
- Tax and bookkeeping sheets peak a little later (February), which extends the window to Feb–Apr.
- Christmas sheets sell in a short Nov–mid-Dec window. That fits gift trackers launched now (late October), not later.
- The broad "budget template google sheets" volume fell 33% year on year. That points to a crowded, possibly cooling head term; long-tail and niche variants are the better bet.

### Gaps
- No eRank CTR, clicks, or month-by-month data for any spreadsheet keyword. The eRank and RankHero pages could not be fetched directly.
- No Google Trends seasonality numbers for "budget template" or "budget spreadsheet". Google Trends was not reachable or returned in search.
- No volume data for: debt tracker, sinking funds, cash envelope, savings challenge, net worth, Christmas budget or gift tracker, Black Friday tracker, meal planner sheet, habit tracker sheet, wedding budget spreadsheet (specifically), ADHD planner sheet, content calendar 2027, Etsy seller spreadsheet.
- 2024 data points are absent. Only 2025–26 RankHero windows surfaced.

## Q2. Which are low-competition opportunities vs saturated?

### Takeaway
RankHero rates even the head budget terms as only "easy to moderate" (difficulty 28–38/100), but listing counts are large: 13K–52K for budget terms and 61K–74K for wedding planning. Prices are compressed below $10. The best openings are niche or job-specific sheets where buyers will pay more: tax and bookkeeping ("tax tracker", difficulty 31, about 5K/mo), Etsy-seller bookkeeping, GLP-1 and weight-loss combo trackers, and premium annual all-in-one budgets. Generic monthly budgets and Christmas gift trackers are saturated.

### Cited Findings
- Budget terms:
  - "budget excel template": difficulty 28, competition 13,071. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-excel-template)
  - "budget spreadsheets": difficulty 32, 22,386 listings. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-spreadsheets)
  - "budget template google sheets": difficulty 30, 20,918 or 26,516 listings, volume down 33% year on year. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-template-google-sheets)
  - "budget tracker spreadsheet": 29,272 or 51,982 listings, CPC $3.39 with high ad competition. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-tracker-spreadsheet)
- Price compression: 73–79% of budget-keyword listings are priced under $10. — [RankHero budget spreadsheet](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-spreadsheet); [RankHero budget tracker spreadsheet](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/budget-tracker-spreadsheet)
- "tax tracker": difficulty 31 ("relatively easy"), ~5,400 volume, Feb peak. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/tax-tracker)
- "wedding planning": 61K–74K listings, difficulty 36. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/wedding-planning)
- Debt and sinking funds: many near-identical bundles at $2–$7, and incumbents hold thousands of reviews (3.4k). — [Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/budgeting_template_with_sinking_funds?page=3)
- Christmas trackers: many near-identical titles across sellers, priced $2–$13. — search snippets of Etsy listings [1246482883](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1246482883/christmas-gift-budget-planner-google), [1585396856](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1585396856/christmas-gift-tracker-google-sheets), [4375371103](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4375371103/christmas-gift-tracker-google-sheets)
- The prior notes rate spreadsheets overall as low–medium competition versus planners and Notion. — `Etsy digital products seasonal demand/evergreen_work_productivity.md`, citing [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/) and [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/most-popular-digital-products-on-etsy)

### Inferences
- **Saturated:** generic monthly budget, generic "budget template google sheets", debt snowball standalone, Christmas gift tracker, habit tracker.
- **Moderate, still winnable with differentiation:**
  - 2027-dated annual all-in-one budget. Dating the product creates fresh, low-competition long-tail terms each year.
  - Paycheck budget.
  - Fitness and weight-loss combo with a GLP-1 angle.
  - Wedding budget dashboard at $10–$25, which carries a higher price tolerance.
- **Lower-competition, higher-price (best bets):**
  - Tax and bookkeeping by profession: Etsy seller, freelancer, cleaning business, handyman. RankHero has pages for "freelancer template" and "handyman invoice", which suggests long-tail demand.
  - Etsy CSV-import seller bookkeeping.
  - Black Friday or holiday-spend trackers. No supply data was found, which may mean thin supply or no demand; it is unverified.
- Winning features seen across the top listings:
  - Dashboards and charts.
  - Excel and Google Sheets compatibility, since some competitors are Excel-only.
  - Reusable or "lifetime" design.
  - Checkboxes and auto-calculations.
  - Unprotected cells, which buyers praised.
  - Guidebooks or tutorials.
  - Bundling several trackers (budget, debt, sinking funds, net worth).

### Gaps
- No precise per-keyword listing counts for niche terms (debt tracker, sinking funds, Christmas budget spreadsheet, Etsy seller spreadsheet). The Etsy result counts could not be fetched.
- Mobile-friendliness as a sales driver is unverified. It appeared only as a feature claim on one bookkeeping listing.

## Q3. Case studies of spreadsheet shops with revenue or sales numbers

### Takeaway
Two well-documented budget-spreadsheet shops show the model works at $100K+/yr: Pretty Arrow (Emily McDermott) and SimplyOrganizedPro (from the prior notes). Several newer shops report thousands of sales. The best-documented shop gets its revenue from $5–$25 products, and its biggest sales come in January from annual budgets.

### Cited Findings
- **Pretty Arrow / PrettyArrowCo (Emily McDermott)**
  - Reported "$250k selling spreadsheets on Etsy in under 2 years" and described as a top 0.1% Etsy seller. She moved from $2 printables to $20 spreadsheets. Most products are $5–$25, and premium products go up to $70. — [Side Hustle Nation (ep. 560, Apr 2023)](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/selling-spreadsheets-on-etsy/)
  - Her own Medium post claims $280k. — [Medium](https://emily-mcdermott.medium.com/how-ive-made-280k-selling-spreadsheets-on-etsy-51b0759a9465)
  - She is quoted as making "$100k a year from my Etsy shop", calls January the biggest month, and says annual budget spreadsheets are the highest-priced items. — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/emily-mcdermott-pretty-arrow/)
  - A third-party case study cites 29,000 budgeting spreadsheets sold and $10,417/month revenue (revenue, not profit). — [Small-Start case](https://small-start.com/en/cases/global-pretty-arrow/)
  - The Etsy shop shows about 30,085 sales. — [Etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/prettyarrowbudget)
  - A Scott Max case study ("$0 to $250k in 2 years selling spreadsheets") cites 29,000+ Etsy sales since 2020, which is likely the same shop but not confirmed. — [Scott Max newsletter](https://newsletter.scottmax.com/p/how-to-go-from-0-to-250k-in-2-years-selling-spreadsheets)
- **SimplyOrganizedPro**
  - About $160K estimated over 2 years, 37K+ sales, 24 products.
  - The best seller is a $1–$2.50 monthly budget with about 8.6K sales, roughly $10K.
  - These are third-party estimates from Feb 2024. — [Goodreads repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24510163-160k-in-2-years-selling-spreadsheet-templates); [Sleepless Yogi](https://sleeplessyogi.substack.com/p/160k-simple-excel-business-how-to?open=false)
- **Newer shops (sales counts only)**
  - LinkDigitalStudio: about 5,960 sales, opened 2025. — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/LinkDigitalStudio)

### Inferences
- There are two viable models:
  - High volume at about $1–$3 (SimplyOrganizedPro).
  - A premium $15–$25 annual budget with January ads (Pretty Arrow).
- For a new shop entering Nov 2026, the Pretty Arrow pattern is the most relevant: a premium 2027 annual budget launched by early December, with Etsy Ads in January.
- LinkDigitalStudio reached about 6K sales within a year of opening in 2025. That suggests new spreadsheet shops can still scale, though the counts are unverified snapshots.

### Gaps
- Revenue figures are self-reported or third-party estimates. There are no audited numbers.
- I found no Reddit r/EtsySellers threads with spreadsheet-specific monthly sales or seasonality.
- No case data was found for tax, bookkeeping, wedding or Christmas spreadsheet shops specifically.
