## Which specific template/planner products peak Nov 2026 – Feb 2027 (demand, competition, price, winning features)?

### Takeaway
The highest-leverage items for this window fall into three groups:
1. **Dated 2027 / multi-year digital planners.** The search proxy peaks in December and stays high into January.
2. **Resume/CV templates.** These have a January peak, with Jan 2026 ≈ +48% vs Dec 2025 on the proxy.
3. **ADHD planners.** The proxy peaked in December 2025, and listing competition is moderate (~50K).

Behind these come vision board kits (very low listing competition relative to search proxy), wedding planners (engagement season Nov–Feb, January planning surge) and Christmas Canva/Corjl templates (Nov–mid-Dec). AI prompt packs show rising search but no verified sales evidence. Holiday Canva social/Black Friday bundles are sold mostly off-Etsy and showed no review evidence.

### Inferences
- **Priority ranking for Nov 2026–Feb 2027 (evidence-weighted):**

  3. ADHD planners. The December peak makes them a gift and New Year item.

  - Hyperlinked navigation.
  - Sizes for each device (e.g., reMarkable Paper Pro/Move, Supernote Manta).
  - Dated from Oct/Nov 2026 to Dec 2027, so buyers can start immediately.
- **OneNote opportunities for this window (inferred, untested by volume data):**
  1. OneNote 2027 planner, dated Nov/Dec 2026–Dec 2027, positioned for work and Microsoft 365 users.
  2. OneNote work/project planner. This is the established OneNote buyer intent, and January is a work-reset moment.

  4. OneNote student planner for the spring semester (Jan–May 2027), as an "academic mid-year" variant.
  5. OneNote wedding planner, for the Jan engagement-planning wave. Notion versions exist, but no OneNote version was found.
- **Saturation and pricing:** GoodNotes is saturated. Bundling "GoodNotes/Notability/OneNote" compatibility is common, so OneNote-native features (sections and tabs, page templates, Outlook-style task tags) are the differentiator.

## What's the January planner/resume spike evidence from 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
On the RankHero proxy:
- **Planners peak in December, not January.** "2026 planner" was 201K in Dec 2025 and 135K in Jan 2026, so January is still about two-thirds of the peak.
- **Resumes peak in January.** "resume template" was 1.22M in Jan 2026 vs 823K in Dec 2025 (+48%).
- **ADHD planner interest peaked in December 2025.**

Evidence for 2024 and earlier years is thin. The Jan 2025 vs Jan 2026 comparison exists only for minor variants.

### Cited Findings
