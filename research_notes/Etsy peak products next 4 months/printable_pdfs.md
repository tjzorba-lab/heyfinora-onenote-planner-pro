## Q1. Which specific printable sub-niches sell or trend for Nov–Dec 2026, and which are saturated? (incl. IP risk)

### Takeaway
Ready-to-play **Christmas party game printables** are a deep, proven niche. The main formats are Family Feud, Most Likely To, multi-game bundles of 20–90 games, and office-party games. Individual listings show review counts from the hundreds to around 10K. Prices are compressed to $1.50–$10, with heavy discounting. **Elf on the Shelf kits**, **kids' activity and coloring packs** and **advent activity cards** are similarly crowded, but premium 24-day kits can reach $25–$87.

What still differentiates a listing:
- an editable format (Canva, Templett or Corjl)
- an audience angle (office/work, medical staff, teens, Christian/Bible, adult vs. family)
- a bundle size that looks bigger than competitors' at the same price

Character themes carry real IP risk. "Grinch" is a USPTO-registered Dr. Seuss Enterprises trademark, and reports in 2026 describe Bluey and Paw Patrol Schedule A lawsuits.

**IP and trademark risk**
- **Grinch:** "GRINCH" is a USPTO-registered trademark of Dr. Seuss Enterprises (Reg. 2328812, since 14 Mar 2000), with newer registrations covering mugs, ornaments and more. This comes from a compliance-vendor blog — [ListingSafe: Is "Grinch" trademarked?](https://listingsafe.app/blog/christmas-trademarked-words-etsy)
- **Bluey and Paw Patrol:** A compliance blog reports that BBC Studios and Spin Master filed **Schedule A** lawsuits against Etsy sellers, which can freeze funds through a TRO. It also says printable party invitations, SVGs and PNG clipart are among the most-targeted categories. These are vendor claims I have not verified against dockets — [ShieldMyShop: Bluey on Etsy 2026](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-06-24-selling-bluey-merchandise-etsy-trademark-copyright-rules); [ShieldMyShop: kids' character lawsuits](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-05-18-selling-kids-character-products-etsy-bluey-paw-patrol-trademark-lawsuits)
- **Counter-notices:** A DMCA counter-notice works only for copyright claims, not trademark claims (vendor source) — [ShieldMyShop: DMCA takedown](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/etsy-dmca-takedown-what-to-do-2026)
- **eRank's own warning:** eRank's December 2025 keyword report says its lists "may contain keywords that are trademarked". In Dec 2025 the US list was led by gift, jewelry gift, personalized gift, christmas and last minute gifts, and included **"wall art" at #8**. Pokémon terms also appeared — [eRank US Dec 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-december-2025/)
- **Other brand names:** "Family Feud" is a game-show brand (Fremantle). Sellers widely use it in titles; one listing shortens it to "Christmas Feud". Using the name carries some trademark risk. This is my inference; I found no takedown evidence.

### Inferences
- **Saturated:** general Christmas game bundles, Family Feud, coloring pages, single Elf letters and generic advent cards. Signs: $1–$5 prices, 40–75% "sales" on everything, 9.9K-review incumbents and many free blog versions.
- **Gaps that look less crowded (audience and format, not theme):**
  - office/HR "virtual + in-person" game packs
  - medical or teacher staff-party editions
  - teen-specific packs
  - Christian/Bible editions (already flagged as "bestselling" variants)
  - Hanukkah game packs (few Etsy results)
  - **"Cozy/bold-easy" adult coloring**
  - Grandma/nostalgic aesthetics (from Etsy's 2025 trend themes, see prior notes)
- **Avoid unlicensed character themes:** Grinch, Bluey, Taylor Swift ("Eras" style), Pokémon, Elf on the Shelf® artwork. "Elf on the Shelf" is itself a trademark of CCA&B, and generic "elf" or "Christmas elf" wording is the safer choice. That last point is my own knowledge, not confirmed in a search result here.

### Gaps
- No keyword volumes for "christmas family feud", "christmas games printable", "elf on the shelf printable" or "christmas coloring pages". eRank, EverBee, Alura and RankHero pages were blocked or not indexed in search.
- Bestseller badges could not be confirmed one listing at a time.
- No Thanksgiving-specific new data was gathered beyond the prior notes.

## Q3. What peaks in February 2027 (Valentine's/Galentine's, Super Bowl, Ramadan, Lunar New Year)?

**Super Bowl LXI falls on Sunday 14 Feb 2027, which is Valentine's Day** (SoFi Stadium). That is the first time this has happened, and it opens a new hybrid niche: "Super Bowl Valentine's party" printables, couples' game-day bingo, and football-themed classroom Valentines.

Classroom Valentines remain the largest February printable segment. One editable set shows ~7.3K reviews at $1.47. "valentine downloads" was #1 (UK) and #2 (Canada) in eRank's February 2026 lists.

Ramadan 2027 starts around 8 Feb and Lunar New Year 2027 falls on 6 Feb, so both land inside the window. Both niches show many listings but thin per-listing reviews, which suggests lower competition.
