# Build a ten-slide carousel that sells templates

The listing should use ten square 2000×2000 px JPGs, all in one consistent style, plus a short, silent screen recording of the Canva edit. The cover is a clean, centred mockup of the invitation that wins the search click. Slides 2–7 remove buyer doubts in order: phone evite, laptop and phone editing in Canva, what you can edit, what's included, how it works, and why buy. A closing slide states plainly "digital download, nothing ships." Etsy recommends at least 2000 px on the shortest side, files under about 1 MB, and up to 20 photos. It also says the first photo should avoid text overlays and collages. That advice conflicts with niche practice, where covers often carry a small "Editable Template" banner, so A/B test the cover. Alt text goes in a per-photo field capped at **250 characters**. Its effect on Etsy search is small and disputed, but it helps accessibility and Google Images. Write each one as a real sentence that carries one long-tail phrase the 13 tags cannot hold. The biggest risk is not image specs. The card was generated with Canva's AI design tool, so it has to clear three rules at once: Etsy requires digital items to be "made and/or designed by you" and requires AI use to be disclosed, Canva bans reselling its templates without substantial changes, and Canva only allows Pro elements when the design is sold as a template link. The seller should rework the AI draft substantially, disclose the AI assistance in the description, and keep every element Free if the listing promises "free Canva account."

## Etsy's specs point to a square 2000 px JPG under 1 MB

Etsy's help pages set the baseline: **at least 2000 px on the shortest side at 72 PPI**. The first photo must be at least 635 px, or it "may show up lower in search" ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop); [Etsy Help art. 208](https://www.etsy.com/uk/help/article/208)). Etsy accepts JPG, PNG, GIF and HEIC. It warns that images over **1 MB "may not finish uploading."** Animated GIFs are not supported, and transparent PNG areas render black. A listing holds **up to 20 photos**, so the old 10-photo habit leaves room unused ([Etsy Help: How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)). Third-party guides agree on 2000×2000 px kept under about 1 MB. They note that Etsy recompresses uploads, so very large files gain nothing ([LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size/); [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size-guide/)). In practice, export sRGB JPGs at 80–85% quality. Leave a little headroom for zoom, but don't go much above 3000 px.

Etsy's own guidance on aspect ratio contradicts itself. One help page says the first photo should be "horizontal (landscape) or square" so the focal point survives the crop ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop?segment=selling)). A sibling article recommends 4:3 and advises against square crops ([Etsy Help art. 208](https://www.etsy.com/uk/help/article/208)). Third-party 2026 guides report desktop thumbnails at 1:1, 5:4 or 4:5 depending on the surface ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size-guide/); [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-optimal-size-aspect-ratio)). Because the ratio is unsettled, a square canvas is the safest choice if all critical content stays within roughly the **central 80%**, with about 250 px of clear margin on every edge. That margin survives both a 4:3 crop and a 5:4 crop. Mobile search thumbnails are only about **170 px wide** ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-seo-for-digital-products)), so headline text must still read at about a tenth of full size. Avoid thin script fonts in banners. Keep corner badges inset, because the app adds an "Instant Download" label to digital listings and crops trim the corners first ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)). After uploading, check the result with Etsy's "Adjust Thumbnail" tool and its thumbnail preview ([Etsy Help: Edit Listing Photos](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016260113-How-to-Edit-Your-Listing-Photos); [Etsy Help: Newly Crafted](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/10603291042967-Newly-Crafted-Etsy-Updates-for-Your-Shop)).

Video is the only image-type lever backed by first-party data. Etsy's July 2020 analysis of **more than five million buyers** found shoppers more likely to buy from listings that have video ([Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-video-tips-166074/)). Listing videos can be **up to 100 MB**, should be at least 1080 px, are cut off after **15 seconds**, and **play without sound** ([Etsy Help: Listing Videos](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053206073-How-to-Add-Listing-Videos)). Etsy's pages disagree on the minimum length (3 or 5 seconds) and on whether a listing gets one video or two. A 10–15 second screen recording of someone clicking the party details and retyping them in Canva, with text captions, fits every version of the rules.

## The cover wins the click; slides 2–7 win the sale

Etsy's August 2025 handbook explains the job of each position. The first image earns the click from search, and the later images close the sale. To optimise the first image for search, sellers should "avoid text overlays, collages, or multi-image thumbnails" ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014), via [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/whats-new-in-etsys-guide-to-crafting-a-winning-listing)). Educators push back for digital products. They argue that a large, high-contrast overlay of **3–5 words** can signal "this is an editable template" in a way a photo cannot ([Printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-thumbnails/); [RatioReady](https://ratioready.com/guides/how-to-create-listing-thumbnails-that-convert)). No public data settles the question. Start with Etsy's rule: a clean flat-lay of the 5×7 card among pine, ornaments and an envelope, with the card clearly dominant. Then test a version with a one-line "Editable Canva Template" banner and compare search click-through and favourites in Etsy Stats, as Printify recommends.

Etsy's Listing Image Requirements also shape the cover. Photos must show the actual finished product. For custom items, placeholder mockups cannot be the main image, but computer-generated mockups are allowed in secondary images ([Etsy Listing Image Requirements](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005)). For a digital invitation, the product is the design itself. The cover should therefore show the real, filled-in invitation, such as "Join us for a Christmas Party, December 13", not a blank frame or lorem ipsum. Etsy's seller policy bans stock photos and images used by other sellers ([Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)). Use only mockup scenes you made or hold a licence for, and avoid AI mockups that misrepresent the design, which eRank specifically warns against ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/mockups-for-your-print-on-demand-etsy-listings/)).

The sequence below synthesises Etsy's handbook, educator guidance and the slide types sold in Etsy mockup kits: "Editable in Canva," "How it works," "What's included," "Product benefits" ([Etsy mockup kit](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1619634053); [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/how-to-boost-your-etsy-listing-conversion-rate-and-sales-with-informational-photos)). No source published a bestseller's exact slide order, so treat the order as a reasoned recommendation rather than an observed standard.

| # | Slide | What it shows | On-image copy (keep to a few large words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Styled flat-lay of the finished 5×7 card, Christmas props, card dominant and centred | None, or test "Editable Template" |
| 2 | Phone evite | Same design on a smartphone screen in 1080×1920 | "Send by text or email" |
| 3 | Laptop + phone editing | Card open in the Canva editor on a laptop, with the date/time text box selected; phone beside it | "Edit in Canva, free account" + "Desktop & mobile" or "Desktop recommended" |
| 4 | What you can edit | Before/after or callout arrows | "Edit all text · fonts · colours"; list fixed elements too |
| 5 | What's included | Icons: PDF with template link, 5×7 print, phone evite, optional back | "Digital download, nothing ships" |
| 6 | How it works | 3–4 numbered steps | "1 Buy & download PDF · 2 Open link · 3 Edit in Canva · 4 Print or send" |
| 7 | Benefits / why buy | Short icon grid | "Instant download · No shipping · Print at home · Message for help" |
| 8 | Detail close-up | Lettering and illustration up close | None |
| 9 | Wording or colour variations | Office party vs family dinner wording, or alternate colourways | Short labels |
| 10 | Matching set / FAQ | Coordinating menu or sign, or FAQ and licence line | "Personal use only" |

Etsy's handbook supports slides 5 and 10. It highlights a seller who added a photo stating plainly that the item is a download, so shoppers understand what they're buying ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/47330319230)). The copy on slides 3–7 should match what Christmas competitors actually promise. Examples include "edit yourself in the free version of Canva… editable 5x7… fully edit all text" ([Etsy 1802126732](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1802126732/cocktail-christmas-party-invitation)) and a phone version at 1080×1920 "great for phone invites" ([Etsy 490489904](https://www.etsy.com/listing/490489904/swimming-pool-party-5x7-and-mobile)). Listings disagree about phone editing. Some say any device works, some say "phone/tablet editing is not available," and others recommend a computer ([Etsy 490489904](https://www.etsy.com/listing/490489904/swimming-pool-party-5x7-and-mobile); [Etsy 4439756278](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4439756278)). Test the template in the Canva mobile app and put the honest answer on slide 3. Fewer surprises mean fewer bad reviews. Keep every slide in one Canva Brand Kit, using one display font, one heavy sans-serif and a palette taken from the card itself ([Brenda Cadman](https://brendacadman.com/how-etsy-sellers-can-use-canva/)). Never put Canva's logo on the slides. Canva's trademark guidelines reserve the logo for "Canva and its authorized licensees" in sales materials ([Canva trademark guidelines](https://s3.amazonaws.com/static-cse.canva.com/pages/media-kit/Guidelines+for+Using+Canva+Trademarks.pdf)). Plain descriptive text such as "Edit in Canva" is widespread, but no source explicitly authorises it, so don't style it to look like Canva's logo or imply endorsement.

## Alt text is a 250-character sentence, not a tag dump

Etsy gives each photo an "Alt text" field. Set it in Shop Manager → Listings → select the photo, or through the pencil icon in the Seller app. It is available when the shop language is English ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/4406604492823-How-to-Add-a-Text-Alternative-to-Your-Listing-Images)). The limit is **250 characters** ([EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/?p=226517); [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/announcements-290/topic/now-available-in-english-text-alternative-22522/)). Shoppers never see it. Screen readers read it aloud, and Etsy says "search engines outside Etsy" use it. If you leave the field empty, Etsy may fill it with **AI-generated text** for US sellers. That text won't mention "editable," "Canva" or "template," so overwrite it on every image. One seller reported that double quotes are stored as `&quot;` and use up hidden characters ([Etsy forum](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/PSA-Alt-text-bug-explained/td-p/140318944)), so leave quotes out entirely.

The effect on ranking is uncertain. eRank says Etsy counts alt text as one factor among many and calls it "unlikely to make a substantial difference" ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/selling-on-etsy-what-is-alt-text/)). Some SEO blogs say it has no effect on Etsy search at all ([Search Endurance](https://searchendurance.com/etsy-seo-for-beginners/)). The clearer benefits are accessibility and Google Images. Google wants descriptive alt text that uses keywords in context and warns that keyword stuffing "may cause your site to be seen as spam" ([Google Search Central](https://goo.gle/2TsmsW3)). Etsy likewise tells sellers to write text that makes sense to a blind shopper hearing it read aloud ([EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/?p=226517)). In practice, describe what the photo shows. Don't start with "image of," because screen readers already announce it ([Front-End Checklist](https://frontendchecklist.io/rules/accessibility/image-redundant-alt)). For infographics, include the slide's text in the alt text, since a screen reader cannot read text baked into an image. Give each image a different keyword variant instead of repeating one phrase ten times ([Cyberclick](https://www.cyberclick.net/numericalblogen/understand-alt-text)).

## Keywords split three ways: title, 13 tags, alt text

No public search-volume figures exist for these phrases. eRank, EverBee and Marmalead keep that data behind logins. Etsy's own market landing pages are the best available evidence of real demand. They exist for "christmas party invitation," "editable holiday party invitation," "christmas party text invite," "christmas party invitations instant download," "xmas party invite" and "printable holiday invitations" ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/christmas_party_invitation); [Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/editable_holiday_party_invitation); [Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/christmas_party_text_invite); [Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/christmas_party_invitations_instant_download)). Etsy's related-searches module also surfaces "Canva Christmas Invitation" and "Elegant Christmas Invitations" ([Etsy 4397535522](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4397535522/christmas-party-invitation-christmas)). Titles are capped at **140 characters**. Tags are capped at **13, of up to 20 characters each** ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-character-limit/)). Long phrases like "editable christmas party invitation" therefore don't fit in tags and belong in the title, the opening of the description and the alt text. A workable title leads with the main phrase: *Editable Christmas Party Invitation Template, Canva Holiday Party Invite, Printable Xmas Dinner Invitation, Digital Evite, Instant Download*. Thirteen tags that each fit under 20 characters: christmas invitation, holiday party invite, xmas party invite, canva christmas, editable invitation, xmas dinner invite, christmas evite, printable invitation, office holiday party, work christmas party, xmas party template, holiday dinner party, and one style tag such as "festive invitation." Timing matters. Marmalead finds Christmas search interest starts climbing around **September 1** and peaks in **late November** ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-holiday-prep/)). A listing that goes live in mid-October 2026 has about five weeks to build sales history before the peak.

## The AI-made card must clear Etsy and Canva rules first

Etsy's Creativity Standards require every digital item to be "made and/or designed by you, the seller" ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276491338090)). Etsy lists "choosing from a manufacturer's templates" and reselling a pre-existing design as grounds for removal ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/34707360607511-Reasons-a-Listing-May-Be-Removed-Under-Etsy-s-Creativity-Standards)). Etsy permits seller-prompted AI work but requires it to be **disclosed in the listing description** ([Etsy Seller Handbook: AI](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004)). Canva sets a parallel rule: existing Canva templates cannot be resold as-is and must be built from scratch or substantially changed in text, fonts, colours and layout ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/); [Canva Content License](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/)). The research did not find Canva terms specific to its AI design tool's output, so the following is inference, not documented policy. AI design tools often assemble their drafts from library templates and stock elements. An unedited AI draft therefore risks counting as both "a manufacturer's template" under Etsy's rules and an unmodified Canva template under Canva's. The safe path is to treat the AI output as a sketch. Rework the layout, typography and palette until the design is clearly your own. Then add a line to the description such as "Design created with AI-assisted tools in Canva and finished by [shop]." Keep the alt text and slides focused on the product, not on how it was made.

Licensing determines what the images can promise. Templates containing **Pro elements may only be sold as Canva template links, never as PDFs or other files**. Free-only designs can be sold either way ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/)). Creating a template link requires a paid plan on the seller's side ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/share-template-link/)). Free-account buyers see Pro elements **watermarked** ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/watermarks-design/)). If slide 3 says "Edit in Canva, free account," every element in the AI-generated design, including any AI-generated imagery the tool inserted, must be Free or your own. Otherwise change the claim to "some elements require Canva Pro." Deliver the template as a PDF containing the link, uploaded in Etsy's "Digital files" slot (up to **5 files of 20 MB**) ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings)). Share it in template-link mode so buyers get their own copy rather than edit access to your master, and add "personal use only, not for resale" to slide 10.

## Conclusion

The image carousel is now largely a commodity. Mockup kits sell the same slide types to every seller, and competitors make near-identical promises. The difference lies in the details that are hard to fake: a cover that shows the real filled-in card clearly at 170 px, a device slide that says honestly whether phone editing works, and a "free Canva account" claim checked element by element. For an AI-generated card, compliance work comes before design work. Redesign the AI draft substantially, disclose the AI use, and confirm every element is Free before shooting any mockups, because an unmodified AI draft or a false "free account" promise can get the listing removed or generate refund claims.

Several things remain unresolved and should be checked in the live tools rather than assumed. These are Etsy's current thumbnail ratio on each surface, whether one or two videos are allowed, whether alt text affects Etsy ranking at all, and how Canva licenses AI-tool output specifically. The best test for this listing is to A/B test a clean cover against a banner cover through the late-November peak. That will show which of Etsy's guidance and the niche's habit actually works for this shop.
