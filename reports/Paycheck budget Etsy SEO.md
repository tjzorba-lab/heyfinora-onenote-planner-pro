## Etsy's 2025 rules favour short, plain, non-repeating titles

Etsy's August 2025 Seller Handbook guidance tells sellers to name the item plainly, put key traits first, **"consider using less than 15 words"**, "try not to repeat words", and move subjective words like "perfect" to the description ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)). Etsy says its search now takes "a more holistic view" of title, tags, attributes, description, first photo and reviews, so the title "doesn't need to do all the work" (same source). eRank reports that Etsy does not penalise older-style titles and calls the guidance optional ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/seo/etsy-title-update-2025-how-to-write-better-etsy-listing-titles/)). Your hard constraints (14 words or fewer, 140 characters or fewer, at most one word repeated, at most twice) are already stricter than Etsy's guidance. The titles below avoid "Instant Download", "PRO" and adjectives like "Ultimate". The digital format is already known to Etsy, and "PRO" is a brand word no buyer searches for, so it belongs in the description and the images.

The honesty rule shapes the platform wording. Because the product is an Excel workbook that only *imports* into Google Sheets, every title pairs "Google Sheets" with "Compatible". The word costs a slot, but it keeps the high-volume "google sheets" token in the title without claiming a native template. That matters because competitors get negative reviews over compatibility, which is why they state caveats like "Google Sheets only NOT Excel!" and "needs Microsoft Excel 365" ([Etsy 1753479207](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1753479207/ultimate-minimalist-paycheck-budget); [Etsy 1396683725](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1396683725/ultimate-budget-by-paycheck-spreadsheet)). Front-loading the main phrase into the first 40-50 characters is practitioner advice, not an Etsy rule ([ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-s-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026)). The recommended title's first 40 characters are "Paycheck Budget Spreadsheet Excel, Googl".

## Conclusion

Honesty and SEO point in the same direction here. The two keywords left out ("debt snowball" and native "Google Sheets template") have the most search volume, and they are also the claims most likely to cause refund requests and 1-star reviews from buyers who expected something else. Because Etsy now ranks the whole listing and counts review and case quality, those reviews would cost more ranking than the keywords could add. The biggest gap the research could not close is real Etsy-internal data. After about 30 days live, check Shop Manager's search-term report and swap any tag earning no visits for one of the listed alternates, starting with "semimonthly budget" or "budget template". For a larger gain, the product could earn the "debt snowball" keyword honestly by adding a snowball/avalanche ordering column to the Debt Payoff tab. That one change would unlock the best demand-to-competition keyword in the niche.
