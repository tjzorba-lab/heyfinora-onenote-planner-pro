# OneNote planners, games aur Valentine's agle 4 mahine jeetenge

**Short jawab:** November 2026 se February 2027 tak Etsy par digital products ki demand teen lehron mein aayegi. **Pehli lehar (ab se 20 December tak)** Christmas party games, Elf/advent kits, Christmas PNG/SVG aur holiday trackers ki hai. Craft files (PNG/SVG) ka season pehle hi chal raha hai, kyunke crafters apna maal banane ke liye 4–8 hafte pehle khareedte hain. **Doosri lehar (26 December se 31 January)** "new year reset" ki hai: 2027 planners, budget spreadsheets, resume templates, vision boards, challenge bingo aur tax trackers. Aap ki OneNote shop ke liye yahi sab se bari lehar hai. Planner search ka proxy **December mein peak** karta hai (Dec 2025 mein "2026 planner" ~201K, Jan 2026 mein ~135K), aur resume search **January mein +48%** uthti hai ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/2026-planner); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/resume-template)). **Teesri lehar (15 January se 14 February)** Valentine's, Galentine's, Ramadan aur Lunar New Year ki hai. Is saal ek naya mauqa bhi hai: reports ke mutabiq Super Bowl LXI **14 February 2027** ko hai, yani Valentine's Day ke din ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/super-bowl-lxi-feb-14-170600490.html)). Neeche 50 products ki table hai. Is mein 16 printable PDFs, 10 spreadsheets, 10 PNG/SVG aur 14 templates/planners hain, aur un 14 mein se 7 OneNote ke hain. Top 10 "abhi banao" products ⭐ se mark kiye gaye hain. Sab se zaroori kaam yeh hai: **27 October 2026 tak Cyber Sale ka signup karein** (yeh tareekh newsletter ke hawale se hai, Etsy se khud confirm karein) aur **2027 OneNote work planner 31 October tak live karein**.

## Har number andaza hai: Etsy asli CTR ya sales public nahi karta

Table parhne se pehle yeh samajh lein. **Etsy asli CTR, search volume ya listing sales public nahi karta.** Is report mein jitne bhi "searches", "CTR" ya "sales" numbers hain, woh eRank, RankHero, EverBee, Listify ya InsightAgent jaise third-party tools ke estimates hain, ya Etsy pages par nazar aane wale review counts hain. Yeh saare sources sirf **search-engine snippets** ke zariye parhe gaye. Etsy, eRank aur RankHero ke pages fetch karne ki har koshish DNS errors aur proxy 403 ki wajah se fail hui. eRank ka "CTR" uska apna clicks-per-search metric hai, aur yeh **100% se upar** bhi ja sakta hai ([eRank Q4 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-in-q4-2025/)). RankHero ki "monthly searches" Google-style volume lagti hain, Etsy ki andar ki search nahi. Misal ke taur par "resume template" ke ~1,000,000 searches/mahina dikhaye gaye hain, jo sirf Etsy ke liye mumkin nahi ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/resume-template)). Is liye in numbers ko sirf **seasonal shape aur relative size** ke liye use karein. Review counts sales ka der se aane wala proxy hain, aur kai snippets mein yeh wazeh nahi tha ke count listing ka hai ya poori shop ka. Tools aapas mein bhi takrate hain: RankHero "digital planners" mein 55% kami dikhata hai, jab ke Listify "digital planner" mein +38% izafa ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planners); [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)). Saath hi kuch khaas keywords ka data mila hi nahi, jaise "onenote planner", "christmas png", "tumbler wrap" aur "super bowl squares printable" ka volume. Table ka Competition column listing counts, discounting aur free alternatives dekh kar meri qualitative raye hai.

### Platform risks: digital-to-print test functional products ke haq mein hai

Sab se bara naya risk Etsy ka **digital-to-print test** hai (September 2026, US/Canada). Eligible digital file khareedne wala buyer usay poster, mug ya apparel par print karwa sakta hai. Yeh printing FYUL (Printify/Printful ki parent company) ki "Ventur Editions" shop karti hai. **Seller ko is ka koi paisa nahi milta, aur opt-out sirf poori shop ke liye hai** ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/43476182453399-How-Printed-Items-From-Digital-Purchases-Work-for-Sellers); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-fyul-pod-test/)). Is ka matlab hai ke wall art aur quote prints jaise products riskier ho gaye hain. OneNote planners, spreadsheets aur editable templates print-eligible nahi, is liye un par is ka asar nahi. **AI** ke rules: AI se bani cheez bechna allowed hai, lekin disclose karna zaroori hai. Design seller ka original hona chahiye, aur **AI prompt bundles banned hain**. Enforcement ki tareekhen sources mein mukhtalif hain ([Growtsy](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy); [PromptlessPress](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products)). Naye Cases policy (9 July 2026, blog ke mutabiq) mein sirf "pasand nahi aaya" wala case "not as described" nahi maana jata, is liye listing mein page count, app compatibility aur "kya shamil hai" saaf likhein ([Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-digital-not-as-described-refunds)). Canva-based design resell karne ke liye sirf font ya color badalna kaafi nahi ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4356604428)).

**Trademark** is season ka sab se khatarnak jaal hai. "GRINCH" Dr. Seuss Enterprises ka USPTO-registered trademark hai (Reg. 2328812) ([ListingSafe](https://listingsafe.app/blog/christmas-trademarked-words-etsy)). Aik compliance blog ke mutabiq BBC Studios (Bluey) aur Spin Master (Paw Patrol) ne Etsy sellers par **Schedule A lawsuits** kiye hain, jin mein TRO ke zariye funds freeze ho sakte hain. Is claim ko maine court dockets se verify nahi kiya ([ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-05-18-selling-kids-character-products-etsy-bluey-paw-patrol-trademark-lawsuits)). "Family Feud" Fremantle ka brand hai, aur "Super Bowl" NFL ka trademark. Sellers is liye **"Super Big Bowl"** aur **"Big Game"** likhte hain ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1855998788/2026-super-big-bowl-game-party-pack)). "Elf on the Shelf" bhi ek trademark hai (yeh meri apni knowledge hai), is liye "Christmas elf" likhein. Etsy aam taur par aik warning deta hai, aur doosri takedown par shop hamesha ke liye band ho sakti hai ([Harper James](https://harperjames.co.uk/article/etsy-copyright-infringement/)). Disney, NFL, Stanley aur Taylor Swift/"Eras" ke naam tags mein bhi na daalein ([TuckerUp](https://www.tuckerup.com/blog/my-etsy-shop-got-shut-down-for-ip-violation-and-im-so-confused-what-that-notice-really-means-and-what-to-do-next/)).

## OneNote ka asal faida: work buyers ka saal January mein shuru hota hai

GoodNotes planner market bhara hua hai. "digital planner goodnotes" ke ~320 monthly searches ke muqable ~117,853 listings hain, aur difficulty 70/100 hai ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-goodnotes)). OneNote ka niche chhota hai lekin wazeh hai. Aik OneNote shop ~7 saal mein **113.9K sales** tak pohanchi hai, prices **$2–$39** hain, aur Etsy ki related searches work ki taraf jhukti hain: "onenote work template", "onenote project management template" aur "onenote weekly planner" ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_digital_planner); [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote)). 2026–2027 ke OneNote ADHD aur "Mid Year Work Planner" listings pehle se maujood hain, is liye OneNote mein bhi competition barh raha hai ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4332596790); [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4379779892)). Sab se bara gap yeh hai ke **kisi bhi tool mein "onenote planner" ka search volume ya seasonality curve nahi mila**. Is liye OneNote ke saare timing faisle general planner curve par mabni hain.

Is se ek wazeh strategy nikalti hai. Aap ka buyer Windows, Surface ya Microsoft 365 wala professional hai, aur us ka "naya saal" office ke Q1 planning ke saath shuru hota hai. Is liye seasonal printables ke peeche bhagne ke bajaye **aik core OneNote work planner** banayein aur us ke gird seasonal add-ons rakhein: holiday planner (#42) abhi BFCM bundle ke liye, goal/vision/budget section (#43) December mein, aur wedding (#41) aur spring semester (#40) editions January ke liye. Har add-on alag listing ho, taake har long-tail keyword ki apni listing ho. "digital planner" sirf secondary tag ke taur par use karein. Design ke 2–3 skins banayein (Cloud Dancer white, frosty blue-silver, Merrymaxxing jewel tones). December mein Pantone 2027 aate hi aik naya skin bhi bana lein. Aakhri faida yeh hai ke OneNote files digital-to-print test mein nahi aatin, aur in mein AI-art ya trademark ka risk bhi kam hai ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/43476182453399-How-Printed-Items-From-Digital-Purchases-Work-for-Sellers)). Lekin listing mein saaf likhein ke file kis OneNote version (Windows desktop, Mac, iPad, web) par chalti hai, kyunke naye Cases policy mein "not as described" ka faisla isi baat par hota hai ([Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-digital-not-as-described-refunds)).

## Conclusion

Is window ka asal sabaq yeh hai ke **sab se zyada paisa sab se zyada search wale keywords mein nahi**. Christmas PNG, tumbler wraps aur generic planners mein $1–$3 ki price war chal rahi hai, 300K–700K listings hain, aur 40K+ sales wali shops ka qabza hai. Jeetne ki asli jagah do tarah ki hai. Pehli: woh products jo kisi **naye mauqe ya kam bhare platform** par hain, jaise Valentine's + Super Bowl ka 14 Feb ka overlap, e-ink devices aur OneNote. Doosri: woh jo **kisi khaas kaam ke liye** hain, jaise tax tracker, office party games aur ADHD planner. Etsy ke naye rules bhi is taraf dhakel rahe hain: digital-to-print test wall art ko kamzor karta hai, AI rules clipart ko mushkil banate hain, aur trademark takedowns character designs ko khatarnak. Is ke muqable OneNote planners aur spreadsheets jaise functional products in teeno risks se bache hue hain.

Aap ke liye amli nateeja yeh hai ke **October–November ka kaam December–January ki kamai tay karega**. 31 October tak OneNote Work Planner live karna aur 27 October tak Cyber Sale signup karna is poore plan ka sab se aham qadam hai. Kyunke kisi tool ke paas OneNote ka apna search data nahi, is season ko apna experiment samjhein: har OneNote add-on ki views, favorites aur sales Etsy Stats mein 60–90 din tak dekhein. Aap ki shop ke liye asli "CTR" yahi numbers hain, aur 2027 ke season mein yahi data RankHero ya eRank ke kisi bhi estimate se zyada kaam aayega.
