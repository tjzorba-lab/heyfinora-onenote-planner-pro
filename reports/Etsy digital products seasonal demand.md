# Christmas, Valentine's aur planners Etsy ka saal chalate hain

**Short jawab:** Pichle 3–4 saal ke Etsy keyword data mein digital products ki sab se bari demand **Q4 (September se December)** mein aati hai. Is mein "christmas" Etsy ke top 2–3 searches mein hota hai, aur Christmas PNG/SVG, gift tags, party games aur agle saal ke planners sab se zyada bikte hain. Q4 ke baad doosra bara spike **Valentine's (January se 14 February)** ka hai. Phir **back-to-school (July–August)**, uske baad Easter (March) aur Mother's/Father's Day (late April–June) aate hain. June–July saal ka sab se slow time hai. Planners, budget spreadsheets, resume templates, shower games aur thank-you cards saara saal bikte hain, aur January mein in ki demand thori barh jati hai. Aap ke liye sab se aham baat: aaj 21 October 2026 hai, to Christmas gift tags, NYE games aur 2027 planners ki listing ka waqt abhi hai, aur Valentine's ki listings November–December mein live honi chahiye. Ek zaroori warning bhi: **Etsy asli CTR, sales ya search volume public nahi karta.** Is report mein jitne bhi "CTR", "searches" ya "sales" numbers hain, woh eRank, EverBee, RankHero ya Listify jaise tools ke estimates hain. Kuch numbers Etsy listing pages par nazar aane wale review counts hain. Saare sources sirf **search-result summaries** ke zariye parhe gaye. Full pages fetch nahi ho sake (DNS errors aur 403), is liye har number ko "andaza" samjhein, pakka data nahi.

## Data ki limits: eRank ka "CTR" asli Etsy CTR nahi hai

Report parhne se pehle yeh samajh lein ke numbers ka matlab kya hai. eRank apna "CTR" metric khud calculate karta hai, yani clicks per search. Yeh **100% se upar** bhi ja sakta hai, aur eRank **125%+** ko "exceptionally high" purchase intent kehta hai ([eRank Q4 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-in-q4-2025/)). EverBee ke sales numbers algorithm ke andaze hain. EverBee khud **~80% accuracy** claim karta hai, lekin aik guide ke mutabiq naye ya kam reviews wali listings par yeh **±50%** tak ghalat ho sakte hain ([EverBee](https://www.everbee.io/product-analytics); [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/how-to-use-everbee)). Tools aapas mein bhi mail nahi khate. Misal ke taur par "digital planner" ke liye RankHero **~694K listings** batata hai aur Listify **1.04M** ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner); [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)). Isi tarah RankHero "digital planners" search mein May 2025 se April 2026 tak **55% kami** dikhata hai, jab ke Listify **+38% YoY** batata hai ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planners)). Etsy apne digital products ka GMS alag se report nahi karta. Poore marketplace ka GMS 2025 mein **4% gir kar $10.5B** hua ([Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://investors.etsy.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001370637-26-000019/etsy-20251231.htm)). Zyada tar keyword data 2025–2026 ka hai, aur 2022–2023 ka data kam mila. Is liye neeche ki rankings ko trend ka ishara samjhein, exact measurement nahi.

## Q4 saal ka sab se bara mausam hai, aur yeh September mein shuru hota hai

eRank ki 2024 annual report mein "christmas" poore saal ki Etsy searches mein **#3** par tha, jab ke 2023 mein **#21** par ([eRank 2024](https://help.erank.com/blog/trend-report/top-etsy-searches-in-2024/)). Q4 2025 mein yeh **#2** par raha, aur "christmas" aur "gift" dono ka eRank-CTR **~145%** tha ([eRank Q4 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-in-q4-2025/)). November 2025 mein "christmas" ka CTR **150%** aur monthly searches **~119K** thin ([eRank Nov 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-november-2025/); [eRank Trend Buzz](https://members.erank.com/trend-buzz/etsy/us/weekly)). Digital products ke liye sab se mazboot signal **"christmas png"** ka hai. October 2025 mein is ka CTR **125%+** tha aur December mein bhi yeh qualifier raha ([eRank Oct 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-october-2025/)). Australia mein "christmas svg" ke **~1,550 searches** aur **140%+ CTR** the ([eRank Australia Nov 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/australias-top-etsy-keywords-november-2025/)). Lekin is niche mein bheer bohat hai. SVG gift-tag bundles **$1–$3** mein bik rahe hain aur 25–50% discount ke saath ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/christmas_gift_tag_svg)).

Timing ke baare mein eRank ki do posts aapas mein mukhtalif hain. Aik kehti hai Christmas searches **September** mein uthti hain, doosri **October** batati hai. Dono ka mashwara aik hi hai: holiday se **~3 mahine pehle** list karein ([eRank holiday tips](https://help.erank.com/blog/tips-for-holiday-success-on-etsy/)). Peak traffic ka andaza **15 November se 10 December** hai. Yeh aik seller guide ka unsourced claim hai ([The First Bloom](https://thefirstbloom.com/10-best-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy-in-q4-2026/)). Mid-December mein "printable gift" aur "digital download Christmas" jaise last-minute searches chha jate hain, kyunke instant download der se khareedne walon ke liye perfect hai ([Snazzy Designs](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)). Black Friday se Cyber Monday ke liye guides **15–30% sale** ka mashwara deti hain, jo **2–3 hafte pehle** set honi chahiye ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/top-selling-cyber-savings-products-on-etsy/)). Halloween ki searches **September** mein spike karti hain. Marmalead ke mutabiq 2025 mein Halloween party printables "exploding" the. Thanksgiving ke digital buyers event ke qareeb, yani **2–3 hafte pehle**, khareedte hain ([Marmalead Fall 2025](https://blog.marmalead.com/top-selling-fall-products-on-etsy/)). Meri raye mein SVG/PNG khareedne wale crafters ko apna physical product banane ka waqt chahiye. Is liye woh printable buyers se pehle khareedte hain. Christmas SVG ko August mein aur gift tags ko September mein list karein.

## Valentine's Q4 ke baad sab se bara spike hai, aur back-to-school doosre number par

Non-Q4 mahinon mein Valentine's sab se aage hai. February 2026 mein UK ki eRank list mein "valentine downloads" **#1** tha ([eRank UK Feb 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-february-2026/)). US Q1 2026 mein "valentines day gift" **#4** par tha, jab ke Q1 2025 mein **#140** par ([eRank Q1 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q1-2026/)). January 2025 mein "galentine gift" ke **~74,000 searches** the ([eRank Jan 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-january-2025/)). Digital products mein classroom Valentine cards sab se wazeh category hai. Yeh **$1.25–$4** mein bikte hain, aur kids ki slang/meme themes (jaise "67") chal rahi hain ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/classroom_valentine_cards)). Etsy khud January ke shuru ya beech mein Valentine's promote karna shuru karta hai ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/marketplace-insights-early-2021-trends/1023216032625)). Is liye listings **December tak** live hon.

Back-to-school doosre number par hai, aur yeh aik lamba season hai. July 2025 mein UK mein "back to school" **#15** par "screamer" tha ([eRank UK July 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-july-2025/)). Etsy Insights ke mutabiq "school" search traffic **28 August wale hafte** peak par tha, aur personalized teacher gifts ki searches **+17% YoY** thin ([Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-whats-trending-for-back-to-school-185685/)). RankHero "printable teacher planner" ke July mein **880** aur August mein **1,000** searches batata hai ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/printable-teacher-planner)). Baqi events chhote hain. "easter" March 2025 mein **#14** par tha ([eRank Mar 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-march-2025/)) aur "fathers day" May 2025 mein **#18** par ([eRank May 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-may-2025/)). "graduation gift" kamzor hua: May 2024 mein **#7** tha, May 2025 mein **#165** ([eRank May 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-may-2025/)). Mother's Day ki demand aksar "gift for mom" jaise general keywords mein chhupi hoti hai, is liye dono keywords use karein ([eRank Q2 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/upcoming-2nd-quarter-2025-holidays/)). Koalanda ki 2023 report mein June aur July average se **~15% aur ~14% neeche** the. Sirf wedding products summer mein ache chalte hain ([Koalanda](https://koalanda.pro/blog/annual-etsy-sales-report-2023)). St. Patrick's, 4th of July, Pride aur tax season ka koi verified data nahi mila.

## OneNote ka mauqa: GoodNotes ki bheer se hat kar work buyers

Broad "digital planner" market bohat bhara hua hai. Is mein **~700K–1M listings** hain, aur "digital planner goodnotes" ke sirf **~320** monthly searches ke muqable mein **117,853 listings** hain (difficulty **70/100**) ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planner-goodnotes)). OneNote ka niche chhota hai lekin wazeh hai. Aik OneNote shop ~7 saal mein **113.9K sales** tak pohanchi, aur prices **$2–$39** hain ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/onenote_digital_planner)). ## Conclusion

Data ka sab se kaam ka sabaq yeh hai ke Etsy par digital products ka saal asal mein **"December, September mein tay hota hai"** wala hai ([Succeed With Content](https://succeedwithcontent.substack.com/p/december-is-decided-in-september)). Jo product peak se 2–3 mahine pehle live nahi, woh peak miss kar deta hai. Sab se zyada search wale niches (Christmas PNG/SVG, gift tags, generic planners, resumes) mein **$1–$3** ki price war chal rahi hai. Wahan jeetna mushkil hai. Asli mauqa wahan hai jahan buyer ki niyat wazeh ho aur listings kam hon: specific app (OneNote), specific kaam (project management, sales), ya specific audience (teachers, classroom Valentines). Aap ki OneNote product ke liye iska matlab hai: seasonal printables se competition karne ke bajaye apna work-planner saara saal chalayein, aur har season mein us ka aik naya version ya bundle listing ke taur par daalein.

Doosra nateeja data ke baare mein hai. Tools aapas mein **55% kami bamuqabla 38% izafa** jitna mukhtalif result dete hain. Is liye kisi bhi aik number par bara faisla na karein. Har naye product ko 60–90 din ka test samjhein aur apni Etsy Stats ke views, favorites aur sales se faisla karein. Yeh numbers hi aap ki shop ke liye asli "CTR" hain.
